Card relations — link cards on the same board as **blocks** / **blocked by** or **relates to**. Blocked cards carry a red indicator on the card face so dependencies are visible while scanning the board, and the card detail panel lists every relation with the linked card and its direction. Archived blockers are excluded from the indicator but stay listed so a dangling link can be removed. Adds `blocker_count` to the card payload and `GET`/`POST`/`DELETE` endpoints at `/boards/{board_id}/cards/{id}/relations/`.
