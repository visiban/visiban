A multi-select lane filter rule with **is not** and two or more values now reads `Pricing regions: none of EMEA, APAC` in its chip, the popover's "Reads as" line and the Filter panel, instead of `not EMEA, APAC`, which could be read as "not EMEA, but APAC". One value still reads `not EMEA`; the rule itself is unchanged. The Filter panel's lane field rows also keep the rule's operator, where they used to show only the value: for example `not gold`, `does not contain beta`, `5 or more`, `up to 5`, or `on or after 11/01/2026` (#2708).
