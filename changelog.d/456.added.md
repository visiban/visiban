Import a Trello board JSON export into a new Visiban board. A two-step wizard (reached from **Import → Import a Trello export**) previews the mapping before anything is created: lists become columns, labels keep their colors and can optionally become swimlanes, checklists, comments (with original timestamps), and archived cards carry over, and attachment links are added to card descriptions. Trello members are matched by username only among people you can already see, and the preview reports just a count of matches. Backed by the new `POST /api/v1/boards/import/trello/` endpoint (`?dry_run=true` / `?confirm=true`). The upload limit is configurable with the new `VISIBAN_IMPORT_MAX_SIZE` setting (bytes, default 25 MB; Helm: `backend.settings.importMaxSizeBytes`), and the bundled nginx body limit is raised from 20 MB to 35 MB to match (#456).
