Harness: an MR's `## Cost of this MR` record now counts the branch's subagents that ran in another session (an MR opened, or a `--round` run, from a different session than the implementer's), instead of silently under-reporting them. `scripts/post-mr-cost.py` also scans the other sessions' subagent transcripts changed since the branch's discovery bound (the earliest author or committer time of its own commits, or its reflog creation, minus 24 hours), claims one only when its home (the worktree it edited or `cd`'d into, never its brief) is the MR's worktree, measures it over the watermark in its own session's state file and advances that watermark (write-ahead, rolled back if the post did not land), and takes every session lock in sorted order so concurrent posts cannot deadlock or claim an agent twice. The other session's orchestrator stays out of the record, which states how many of its tokens are unclaimed. `subagent-tokens.py` and `est-cost.py` gain `--also-session <sid>` (its directory must resolve inside the projects directory), and `cost-table.py` gains `--other-sessions S:A:T` (port of macrodream/blueprint#389).
