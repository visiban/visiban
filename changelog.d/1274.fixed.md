- `AdminIPRestrictionMiddleware` now matches CIDR ranges in `DJANGO_ADMIN_ALLOWED_IPS` (e.g. `10.0.0.0/8`), not just exact IPs, for both IPv4 and IPv6 — bringing it in line with the Nginx allowlist that accepts the same `backend.settings.adminAllowedIPs` Helm value (#1274)
- Invalid, host-bits-set (e.g. `10.0.0.5/24`), and very broad (including `/0`) `DJANGO_ADMIN_ALLOWED_IPS` entries now log a warning at startup (#1274)
