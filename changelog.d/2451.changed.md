The board Analytics request (`analytics/`) now reads the window's cards and movements once instead of twice: the dwell heatmap's own figures (`avg_days_per_column`, velocity, stalled cards) are derived from the same history load as its flow-service dwell, which also drops four per-card movement subqueries from the card read. Two tie orders are now fixed by card or movement id where the database used to choose: cards tied on swimlane and position list in card-id order in `stalled_cards`, and when two of a card's movements share a `moved_at` (a bulk move or an import), the column credited with the stay in `avg_days_per_column` (and the `board_medians` built from the same stays) is decided by movement id, the `(moved_at, id)` order the flow metrics already use (ADR #1532).
