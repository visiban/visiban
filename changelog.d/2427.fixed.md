`GET /boards/{id}/analytics/` reads its heatmap cards and movements as plain rows instead of model instances, roughly halving the peak memory of a long-period request on a board with a long history (`days=365` on a board with 2,400 active cards and three years of archived history, measured on SQLite through the DRF test client: 104 MB to 57 MB; the issue's own Postgres baseline was 137 MB, and this was not re-measured on Postgres). The response is unchanged. This does not by itself bring a 12,000-card board within its memory target.
`GET /boards/{id}/analytics/` also releases the flow service history it loaded for the heatmap dwell and blocked-time sections before it loads its own cards and movements, so the two loads no longer sit in memory at once. The response is unchanged. Peak memory on a 12,000-card board with a year of history was not re-measured.
