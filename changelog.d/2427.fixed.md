`GET /boards/{id}/analytics/` reads its heatmap cards and movements as plain rows instead of model instances, roughly halving the peak memory of a long-period request on a board with a long history (`days=365` on a board with 2,400 active cards and three years of archived history: 104 MB to 57 MB). The response is unchanged. This does not by itself bring a 12,000-card board within its memory target.
