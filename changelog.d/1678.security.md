Emailed group and board invites are now bound to the address they were sent to. Visiban stores only a keyed fingerprint of the address. Redeeming the invite requires an account with that address, both when joining and when creating an account from the invite; under `EMAIL_VERIFICATION=mandatory` the address must be verified. A mismatch is refused with `invite_recipient_mismatch` and the invite stays unused. This also applies to someone who is already a member: opening an unused emailed invite sent to a different address now returns `403` instead of being accepted. Shareable invite links, site invite links, and emailed invites sent before upgrading are unchanged (#1678).
