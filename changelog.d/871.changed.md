Card urgency badges and the standalone due-date pill now use a flag (overdue) or stopwatch (due soon) icon instead of the ⚑/⏱ text characters, so they take the badge color on every platform and read correctly to screen readers (the overdue checklist-item count on the card face uses the same flag icon; an overdue badge announces "Overdue"). The standalone due pill now takes the danger tone when overdue and the amber on-tint tone when due within the soon window (previously plain text-danger or fg-tertiary), and shows a stopwatch glyph when due soon, so it is slightly wider. The untinted due-soon text is darker amber (7.31:1 on a light card, was 3.56:1).
