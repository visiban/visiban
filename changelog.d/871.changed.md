Card urgency badges and the dense due-date pill now use a flag (overdue) or stopwatch (due soon) icon instead of the ⚑/⏱ text characters, so they take the badge color on every platform and read correctly to screen readers (an overdue badge announces "Overdue"). The untinted due-soon text is darker amber (7.31:1 on a light card, was 3.56:1).
