An open **Board settings → Webhooks** tab now follows changes made elsewhere without a reload: another admin creating, editing, rotating, turning off or deleting a webhook, or the dispatcher seeing it fail, recover or turning it off. Changed rows are tagged **Changed just now**, a webhook deleted while open says so with **Back to all webhooks** (the notice takes focus and is announced to screen readers), and the list is also re-read when the window regains focus and when the board's live connection reconnects; an open form, confirmation or unstored secret is never replaced. Carried by a new admin-only WebSocket event, `webhook.changed`, with ids and codes only (never the URL or a secret), which is not stored in the change feed and cannot be subscribed to by a webhook (#2057).
