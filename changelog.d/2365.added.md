- Card History now shows a card moving between the sub-columns of a split stage ("Moved to Review in Build"), including moves caused by a stage-settings change, with a new **Sub-column moves** filter; the card peek sheet's history lists them too. The card timeline API (`GET /api/v1/boards/{board_id}/cards/{id}/timeline/`) returns these as a new `kind: "gate"` entry with an optional `gate` filter group and `counts.gate`, and move entries now carry `from_gates_passed`, `from_gates_open` and `to_gates_passed`. Requests that name their `event_types` groups get the same response as before (#2365).
