The group list's `member_preview` no longer joins every direct membership of a listed group to its user before keeping the first five. A new `(group_id, joined_at, id)` index, built concurrently, feeds the ranking in order, and only the kept members' users are loaded. On a 5,000-member group the ranking reads 81 buffers instead of about 17,900. The list now runs two bounded queries for the preview instead of one, still flat in the number of groups.
