Flow metrics now split the time a card spends in a gated stage into work and wait time (#1102): the sub-columns before the last count as work and the last sub-column counts as wait, read from the recorded gate history. Cycle-time and dwell samples gain `wait_days` and `work_days`, dwell columns gain `work_percentiles` and `wait_percentiles`, and the dwell heatmap's swimlanes gain `throughput_wait_avg_days_per_column` and `age_wait_avg_days_per_column`. All are additive; no existing value changes, and a stage with no sub-columns reports no split.
