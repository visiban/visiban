Notifications can now be delivered by **email** as well as in the app, for four events: a card assigned to you, an @mention in a comment or description, a card due within 24 hours, and a card you own moved to another column. Each event has its own per-user opt-in under **Settings → Notifications**, and **every one of them defaults to off** — upgrading an instance that already has SMTP configured does not start emailing anybody, and nothing is sent until a user opts in for themselves. Delivery uses plain SMTP through the existing email configuration (`EMAIL_HOST`/`EMAIL_PORT`/`EMAIL_USE_TLS`/`DEFAULT_FROM_EMAIL`, or **Admin → Settings → Email**) with no third-party service; two new variables, `NOTIFICATION_EMAIL_ENABLED` and `NOTIFICATION_EMAIL_TIMEOUT`, give operators an instance-wide off switch and a send timeout. A mail failure never breaks the action that caused it. The due-date notification itself is also new — earlier releases documented it and shipped its preference toggle, but nothing created it; it is produced by a new idempotent `notify_due_soon` management command (#356).
