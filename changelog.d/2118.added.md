New `POST /api/v1/boards/{id}/webhooks/{id}/end-overlap/` ends a secret rotation overlap early without issuing a new secret: from then on every delivery carries one signature, from the unchanged current secret, and the stored previous secret is deleted. Session-only like `rotate`, idempotent, and it does not change the webhook's owner. A dispatcher run in progress drops the second signature from its next delivery (#2118).
