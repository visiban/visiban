Planned children: Add child takes an optional **Needed** column on the parent's board. The child waits in To do with its due date inactive (left out of Overdue, Due soon and Stale on every surface, the share link and digests included) until the parent reaches or skips past that column, or someone pulls it. Each change is recorded in the child's history, a deleted column resets it to Needed: Now, and `POST .../cards/<id>/needed/` changes it by hand (#2371).
