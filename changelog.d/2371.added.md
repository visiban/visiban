Planned children: Add child takes an optional **Needed** column on the parent's board. The child waits in To do with its due date inactive (left out of Overdue, Due soon and Stale wherever the server works them out, including the share link's Stale flag, the stale digest, the due-soon notification and lane health, and in the board view) until the parent reaches or skips past that column, or someone pulls it. Each change is recorded in the child's history, a deleted column resets it to Needed: Now (its Stale clock restarts then, as it does when someone sets Now by hand), and `POST .../cards/<id>/needed/` changes it by hand; a released child's card says whether the parent moved on or the plan was called off (`needed_now_reason`) (#2371).
