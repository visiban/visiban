`analytics`: `throughput_avg_days_per_column` and `age_avg_days_per_column` are computed from visits. A swimlane-only move no longer splits a visit or resets a card's age, archiving closes a visit and restoring opens a new one, and a visit keeps its true start however long ago it began. Columns are matched by identity, so a column deleted and recreated under the same name no longer inherits the old column's visits or done flag.
