Hard-deleting a card now keeps its activity trail (field changes, checklist and block events, and the record that a comment was posted), as movements have since 1.2: the rows keep the card's UID and title, are append-only and view-only in the Django admin, and are removed only with their board or by `purge_card_history`, which now erases them too. Operator-visible change: `purge_card_history --include-live-cards` now also erases a live card's activity trail, so its timeline loses those entries; in 1.2 the command never touched activity records. Comments are card content and are still deleted with the card.
