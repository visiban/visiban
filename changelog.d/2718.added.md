Entry requirements on a number field can now set an upper bound: **is less than** and **is at most** join **is more than** and **is at least**, so a column can say "Win probability is at most 20%". The value box shows the field's number format (such as `%` or `$`), and the "Needs:" line names the bound in that format. API: `lt` and `lte` operators on `PUT .../columns/{id}/entry-requirements/` (#2718).
