The one-time migration of browser preferences saved under the pre-1.2 key names (`sidebar-collapsed`, `sidebar-groups-expanded`, `visiban-theme`, `visiban-theme-synced`) has been removed. Users upgrading directly from 1.1 or earlier to 1.3 find their sidebar preferences reset to the defaults once, while their theme is taken from their account; anyone who went through 1.2 is unaffected (#1859).
