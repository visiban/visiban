The board JSON export carries card relations (blocks / relates to) in a new top-level `relations` list (`{from, kind, to}`, indexes into the file's `cards`), and JSON import restores them after creating the cards. Invalid or dangling entries are dropped rather than failing the import, and with the cards option off they are counted in the new `import_summary.skipped.relations`. The sample boards now include relations, with at least one active blocker each.
