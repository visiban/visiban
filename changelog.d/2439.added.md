Board admins can choose where each swimlane field shows on the lane band: **Band** (always visible, up to 6), **Details** (behind "Show N more", up to a per-board limit of 4, 6, 9, 12 or 16), or **Hidden** (listed last in the lane details under "Hidden on band"). Board settings → Swimlane fields gains the Show control, counters, the details limit, a field search and a live preview. Existing boards switch to these tiers at once: the first two fields are Band and the rest Details (#2658). The API adds `band_visibility` to swimlane field definitions and `lane_details_cap` to boards (#2439).
