Board import can now leave parts of an export out. After choosing a Visiban JSON or CSV file, an **Include** list lets you uncheck cards, labels, comments, checklist items, or card history; board structure (name, columns, swimlanes) is always imported. When something is left out, the new board opens with a notice of what was skipped. The `POST /api/v1/boards/import/` endpoint accepts a matching optional `options` field and adds an `import_summary` to its response.
