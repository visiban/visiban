Board export (CSV and JSON) reads its card rows, and their related history rows, from the database in chunks of 500 instead of all at once. The exported file is still built whole in memory (the JSON document with every card's entry, and the CSV body) and is not streamed. No card appears twice or is skipped when cards move while the export runs, and there is no new size limit. A board of fewer than 500 cards reads its cards in the same queries as before.
