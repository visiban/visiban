Added `backend-diff-coverage` and `frontend-diff-coverage`, MR-only CI jobs that measure test coverage of only the lines an MR changes (via `diff-cover`, reusing the existing `coverage.xml` / `cobertura-coverage.xml` reports — no test re-run), blocking at 80% of changed lines. The existing aggregate coverage gates (`backend-test-coverage`'s 90%, the frontend's vitest thresholds) measure the whole codebase and can absorb a fully untested new feature without moving the total; these new jobs close that gap and print the uncovered line numbers directly in the job log (#1076).
