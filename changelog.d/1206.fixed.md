- Fixed logging in with an email address. The login form has always said "Username or email", but only the username was ever accepted. The `username` field of `POST /api/v1/auth/login/` (and the optional `email` field) now accepts either the username or the account's email address, matched case-insensitively. A username always takes precedence, and an email address shared by more than one account logs into none of them. Failed attempts by username and by email now count toward a single per-account lockout, so switching identifiers no longer doubles an attacker's guesses.
