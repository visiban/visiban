Board settings no longer stays locked forever when a webhook create or secret rotation never gets an answer. After 20 seconds the Webhooks tab offers **Leave anyway** with a warning that the change may still go through and its secret can't be shown afterward; once you leave, the tab says a webhook or rotation may have landed (also after closing Board settings or reloading, until you dismiss it) and re-reads the webhooks, so **Old secret still signed until…** reflects the server. Screen readers hear that notice when it appears, and focus moves to a heading rather than being lost when **Leave anyway** or **Dismiss** goes away. The request itself is never canceled, and no global client timeout is added (#2127).
