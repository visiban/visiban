Flow metrics on the Analytics tab: cycle time (scatter with p50/p85/p95 lines), weekly throughput, a cumulative flow diagram with its bottleneck, and WIP aging. Each chart has a table view and a click-through to the cards, transitions and owners behind it. Backed by `GET /api/v1/boards/{id}/metrics/{cycle-time,throughput,cumulative-flow,wip-age}/`, from one flow metrics service that follows the flow metric definitions record.
