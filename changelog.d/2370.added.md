Children mode API: `GET /api/v1/boards/{id}/children-view/` returns, per viewer, every lane's child rollup (in progress, done, doing, to do, blocked, and a count of children on boards you can't open) and the board's parent rows with their children, 20 per page, blocked rows first (#2370).

Children mode on the Board tab: a "Show: Cards | Children" control on boards where cards can have children shows every parent's child tasks by lane, with lane totals, blocked counts, planned-children folds ("2 in progress · 2 planned", "2 planned · Show", "· 1 with only planned work"), locked rows for boards you can't open, and a shareable `?show=children&lane=<lane id>` link. The children-view API adds `planned` and `planned_only` counts (#2370, #2550).

Ways into Children mode: a "Children 12 in progress" action beside Focus on every lane band, an "All children in {lane} (N in progress)" link at the end of a parent's Children tab, and a "Parent lane" filter on child boards (`f_parent_lane`, kept in saved filters as `parentLaneFilter`). In Children mode the lane band keeps Focus and the admin menu, and the child-board column heads show whole-column counts and limit meters (#2370).

Children mode Move and live updates: members of a child board can move a child to another column from Children mode through the Move picker (column only, with the child board's limits), counts update live, rows never re-sort under keyboard focus (a "N changes · Show" button applies the new order), and the Children lane action appears on phone lane bands. In a parent's Children tab, each card's Move and Unlink buttons are now reached with the arrow keys instead of being extra Tab stops (#2370).

Children mode on phones: two-line tinted lane bands, one child board and one of its columns at a time with the phone board's column buttons, parent names as headings inside the column ("Nothing blocked" when a parent has none there), and an "Onboarding · 2 children · Columns ›" switch for a lane's other child boards; nothing scrolls sideways (#2370).

Move API: `POST /api/v1/boards/{board_id}/cards/{id}/move/` accepts an omitted or `null` `swimlane_id` and keeps the card in its current lane, read under the move lock, so Children mode and the Children tab move a child between columns without putting back a lane someone else just changed; the MCP `move_card` tool does the same when `to_swimlane_id` is omitted. Changing a child's Needed now also sends `card.children_changed` to the parent's board, and Children mode re-reads on parent-board column reorders, deletes and renames and on a parent's title or assignee edit (#2370).
