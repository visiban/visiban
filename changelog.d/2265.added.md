Break flow metrics down by swimlane, label or a dropdown or checkbox card custom field (#2265): `metrics/cycle-time`, `metrics/throughput`, `metrics/wip-age` and `metrics/dwell` take an optional `group_by` (`swimlane`, `label`, `custom_field` with `group_by_field_id`) and then add a `grouping` envelope with up to five named groups plus "Other", per-group aggregates and an `excluded` count, and group keys on each sample. Without `group_by` the responses are unchanged.
