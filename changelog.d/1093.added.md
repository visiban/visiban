CI gate scripts now follow a `--self-test` house rule: `osv-severity-gate.sh` and
`assemble-changelog.sh` gained self-test modes, `check-migration-numbering.sh`'s existing
self-test is now actually invoked by its CI job, and a new `gate-selftest-parity` meta-gate
derives the list of bespoke gate scripts from `.gitlab-ci.yml` itself and fails if any lacks
a working, wired self-test. See `docs/development/ci-gates.md` for the convention, the
documented exemptions, and known gaps (#1093).
