Removing a member from a group now also closes their open connections to a board placed under the group's subtree while the removal was in progress, resolves their remaining board roles in a fixed number of queries instead of one lookup per board, and writes the eviction events in transactions of at most 50 boards, for at most 500 boards per removal (a removed user's connections to any further boards close at the periodic access re-check, and the skip is logged). A failure while writing those events no longer answers the completed removal with a server error or skips the group-channel event, and a subgroup created while the removal was in progress now also receives that event.
