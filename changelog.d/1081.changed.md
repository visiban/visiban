- Indexes and constraints are now built without locking the table. Every index Visiban adds from 1.2 onward uses `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY`, and every check constraint is added `NOT VALID` and validated separately, so `migrate` no longer takes an `ACCESS EXCLUSIVE` lock on `cards` — previously a full read/write outage for the duration of an index build, and the blocker to documenting a zero-downtime upgrade. New `visiban.db_operations` wrappers provide the pattern (falling back to plain operations on SQLite installs), and a new `manage.py check_migration_concurrency` check runs in CI and fails on any migration that regresses to a blocking build via `AddIndex`, `AddConstraint`, `db_index=True`, `unique=True`, `AlterUniqueTogether`, or raw `CREATE INDEX`. The pattern and the decision to leave the fourteen pre-1.2 migrations untouched — with a documented build-by-hand procedure for operators replaying them against a populated database — are in the new [Database migrations](https://gitlab.com/visiban/visiban/-/blob/main/docs/development/database-migrations.md) guide (#1081).
