Card actions menu and ⌘K card commands: right-click a card (or focus it and press `Menu` / `Shift+F10`, or tap **⋯ More actions** on a touch screen) to open, copy a link to, move, assign, reprioritize, block or unblock, archive or delete it without opening it; a card inside the selection acts on the whole selection. On a board, the command palette lists the same commands (except Delete) for the open, focused or selected card, and `→` on a card result shows that card's commands. Items a role can't use stay listed with the reason. A right-click on a card no longer opens the add-card input; right-click empty cell space for that (#2059).
