The ⌘K / Ctrl+K command palette now works on every authenticated route (Dashboard, Groups, Settings, Admin, and every Board sub-tab) rather than only on the main Board view. The palette's scope and placeholder copy adapt per surface — board cards on board routes, a boards-only jump on Dashboard/Group, a general nav list on Settings/Admin — and the "ghost dialog" bug where pressing ⌘K on a Board sub-tab left the palette visible after navigation has been eliminated.
