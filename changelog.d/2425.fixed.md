Flow metric endpoints (`metrics/cycle-time`, `throughput`, `cumulative-flow`, `wip-age`, `dwell`, `attention`, `swimlanes`) no longer replay the board's whole lifetime history for every request: they load only the cards live now or active since the range opened, so their cost no longer grows with the board's age. Every number is unchanged. On a board with three years of archived history, peak memory per request dropped from about 150 MB to about 20 MB.
