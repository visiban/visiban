Flow metric endpoints (`metrics/cycle-time`, `throughput`, `cumulative-flow`, `wip-age`, `dwell`, `attention`, `swimlanes`) no longer replay the board's whole lifetime history for every request: they load only the cards live now or active since the range opened, so memory and replay time follow the board's recent work rather than its age. Every number is unchanged. `cumulative-flow` still reads every older card's movements once to place finished work in the done band, so its latency still grows with the board's history (about 6.6 s on a three-year-old board). Measured on SQLite through the DRF test client (not measured on Postgres) on one board of 2,400 active cards plus three years of archived history (31,200 cards, 230,288 movements), the narrowed endpoints dropped from about 7.8 s and 150 MB to about 1.2 s and 19 MB each.
