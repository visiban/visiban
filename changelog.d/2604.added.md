Site admins can set a **Docs address** in Site admin → General → Help links to point the in-app "?" and "Learn more" links at their own docs mirror at runtime, including on the prebuilt release images. It takes precedence over the build-time `VITE_DOCS_BASE_URL`, accepts `http://` or `https://` only, and is published as `docs_base_url` on `GET /api/v1/auth/site-config/` and `GET`/`PATCH /api/v1/admin/settings/`.
