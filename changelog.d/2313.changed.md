Overdue now follows one rule on the board and on the server: a card is overdue when its due date is before today in the instance time zone (`INSTANCE_TIMEZONE`, UTC by default) and it is not archived or in a done column. The card face's overdue badge, border, status line, lane status and Show chips previously called a card overdue from midnight UTC at the start of its due day, and kept the cue on finished cards; a card due today now reads "Due soon" until midnight. `GET /api/v1/auth/site-config/` adds `instance_timezone` so the web app can apply the rule. The `?overdue=` card filter is unchanged.

Three overdue signals still follow each person's own time zone, pending #871: the standalone due pill's color and glyph (shown only when no Overdue or Due soon status carries the date), and the "Due today" and "Due this week" filters. The due-soon notification now also skips cards in a done column. Proposed, pending owner confirmation: a done or archived card is never overdue, a card due today reads "Due soon" until midnight in the instance zone, and `instance_timezone` is served on the anonymous site-config endpoint.
