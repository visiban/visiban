The **Comment on a watched card** notification preference now works: with it turned on, you are notified in-app when someone else comments on a card you created or are assigned to. Previously the toggle was saved but no notification was ever created. You are not notified about your own comments, twice for a comment that also @mentions you, or after losing access to the board. A new `comment_added` notification `action_type` is added. Commenting with an @mention on a card whose title is near the 500-character limit no longer returns a server error on PostgreSQL (#1277)
