A multi-select lane filter rule can now hold several values: **is** matches a lane with any of them and **is not** a lane with none of them, picked from a checklist, with chips such as `Lanes · Pricing regions: EMEA, APAC`. Saved filters and shared links gain an optional `values` list (at most 100 entries, the most choices a field may have); a rule with one value keeps its `value` shape and loads unchanged, and older clients ignore a several-value rule rather than applying part of it (#2659).
