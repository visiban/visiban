Planned child cards are now released correctly on every column write. A parent card moved while a column reorder was in progress releases its planned children by the column order the reorder saved, instead of the order read before it, which could release a child too early or leave it planned (#2635). Column edits in the Django admin now follow the same rules as the board: changing a column's position releases planned children whose Needed column it puts at or before the parent's column, and deleting a column, or moving it to another board, sets its planned children to "Needed: Now" with a history entry and restarts their Stale clock (#2637).
