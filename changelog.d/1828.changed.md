The board members endpoints now refuse to remove or demote the board owner's membership: `DELETE /api/v1/boards/{id}/members/{owner_id}/`, and a `POST /api/v1/boards/{id}/members/` that would give the owner a role below `admin`, return `400` with `code: board_owner_protected`, as groups already refuse for their owner. Before, they succeeded but changed nothing the owner could do and only hid the owner from the roster. `POST /api/v1/groups/{id}/transfer-ownership/` now refuses a deactivated new owner with `400`, as the Django admin transfer already did (#1828).
