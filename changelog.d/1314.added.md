Signing in with Google, GitHub, GitLab, or SSO using an email that already has a Visiban account now explains what to do instead of failing: the login page asks you to sign in with your password (or the provider your account already uses), then offers once to connect the new provider for next time. Settings → Security has a new **Connected accounts** section to connect or disconnect providers, which never lets you remove your last way to sign in. Backed by the new `pending_connect_provider` user field and `GET`/`DELETE /api/v1/auth/me/connected-accounts/` and `DELETE /api/v1/auth/me/pending-connect/` endpoints.
