Lane filters: a **Lanes** rule builder in the board filter bar hides swimlanes by their own dropdown, number (range) and date (range) field values, with per-pair And/Or toggles, one level of groups, and a plain-language "Reads as" line. Lane rules persist in the URL (`f_lanes`), in browser filter state and in saved filters (`state_json.laneFilter`); focus mode pauses them; admin-only fields are offered to admins only. The filter row's landmark is now named "Board filters".
