Every authentication entry point (REST, MCP, the board and group WebSocket consumers, invite links and the OAuth adapter) now applies the account-state and invite-creator rules through one shared module, `visiban.authorization`, and a CI test discovers the entry points and fails when a new view, consumer or transport does not use it. No check is weakened, and responses and WebSocket close codes are unchanged (an inactive account is still refused at authentication: `4001` on a WebSocket, `401` on MCP). The WebSocket handshake and MCP now also evaluate the inactive-account gate themselves, as defense in depth.
