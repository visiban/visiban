Every authentication entry point (REST, MCP, the board and group WebSocket consumers, invite links and the OAuth adapter) now applies the account-state and invite-creator rules through one shared module, `visiban.authorization`, and a CI test discovers the entry points and fails when a new view, consumer or transport does not use it. No check is weakened; WebSocket connect and MCP additionally refuse an inactive account (defense in depth). No change for existing clients.
