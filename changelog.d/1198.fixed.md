- Fixed CI jobs hitting Docker Hub's anonymous-pull rate limit (`429 Too Many Requests`), most visibly on the `helm-install`/`helm-netpol` drills' fresh, credential-less `docker:dind` daemon. Docker Hub image references in `.gitlab-ci.yml` now resolve through the GitLab Dependency Proxy, and the backend/frontend Dockerfiles take a `BASE_REGISTRY` build arg (default `docker.io/library`, so local and contributor builds are unaffected) that CI points at the proxy. Requires the Dependency Proxy to be enabled for the `visiban` GitLab group.
