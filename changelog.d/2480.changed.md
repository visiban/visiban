The public shared board, "Page not found" and "This board/group isn't available" pages use the shared look. A shared board has a cut-down header with a "Shared view, read-only" pill and Sign in, says it is a read-only snapshot, shows a skeleton while loading, names each card's priority for screen readers, and on phones shows one column at a time. The not-found and unavailable pages offer Go to Home and Search boards (⌘K).
