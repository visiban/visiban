After deleting a custom field in Board Settings (card or swimlane fields), keyboard focus now moves to the "+ Add field" button instead of being lost, whether the delete started from the row's ✕ or from the field editor's Delete field button.
The "Delete board?" dialog in the board selector got the same fix: after a confirmed delete, focus moves to the "Your Boards" heading instead of being lost.
The "Delete board?" dialog on the Dashboard and the "Delete column?" dialog on the board view got the same fix: focus returns to the "My Boards" heading and the board region respectively after a confirmed delete.
