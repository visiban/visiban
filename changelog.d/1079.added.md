A `serializer-ts-parity` CI gate now compares the generated OpenAPI schema against the hand-written TypeScript interfaces in `frontend/src/types/index.ts`, so a serializer field added, removed, or retyped without its frontend counterpart fails the pipeline instead of drifting silently. It covers `Board`, `Card`, `User`, `BoardMembership` and `BoardUser`, checking field presence in both directions plus type family, nullability, and enum membership. Known mismatches are recorded as self-invalidating suppressions that fail the gate once the drift they cite is fixed, so a suppression cannot outlive its reason. See `docs/development/serializer-ts-parity.md` (#1079).
