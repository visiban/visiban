Board: a full board refresh (on reconnect, tab focus, a version conflict or **Refresh board**) no longer brings back a column or swimlane deleted while the refresh was in flight. Before, the column or swimlane came back together with its cards and stayed until the next full refresh, because no later event removes it again. Every other change to the board that arrives during a refresh is now applied again on top of the server's answer, in the order it arrived: columns and swimlanes created, edited or reordered, labels created, renamed or deleted (including on cards), member changes, custom field definitions, and board settings, share link and star state. A column created during a refresh therefore stays, so a card created in it is no longer left without a column, and the archived-card count is re-read after the refresh when a column or swimlane was deleted during it (#2130)
