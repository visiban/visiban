Turning off "Cards on this board can have children" is no longer refused while cards on the board have children: those cards keep their children and can still get more (Add child, Link existing), no other card there can, and they keep their progress rollup. The Link existing picker marks a card that has children of its own as "has children of its own". Board settings now asks first with a "Turn off children on <board>?" confirm (Keep it on / Turn off) when cards on the board have children, instead of the old "Can't be turned off" blocked state.
