Fixed floating promises in `components/Board` that silently swallowed failures: renaming a column or swimlane, saving a column, adding a card, loading archived cards, loading groups to move a board, saving card-aging settings, copying a board's share link, enabling/disabling board sharing, and changing a member's role, moderator status, or removing them now surface an error and roll back to the last known-good state instead of failing with no feedback (#1373). Blurring the stale-card-threshold or warning-percentage fields with no actual change no longer sends a redundant save request.
