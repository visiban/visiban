Card attachment uploads are now bounded per card and per user: a card holds at most 100 attachments and 1 GiB by default (`ATTACHMENT_MAX_PER_CARD`, `ATTACHMENT_MAX_BYTES_PER_CARD`, `0` = no limit), and uploads have their own per-user rate limit, 120 per hour by default (`ATTACHMENT_THROTTLE_UPLOAD_RATE`). An over-quota upload gets `400` `attachment_quota_exceeded` and stores nothing; the quota is checked under the card row lock, so concurrent uploads cannot both take the last slot. The upload content check now requires a binary file's signature to match its declared type and scans the whole body of a plain-text or CSV file for HTML markup, not only the first 4 KB. Helm exposes the three settings under `backend.settings` (#2007). The card upload form shows the server's reason for a refusal (over quota, file type mismatch) and a rate-limit message on `429`, instead of a generic "Could not upload attachment." (#2007).
