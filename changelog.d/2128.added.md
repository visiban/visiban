Record each merge request's cost in the contributor harness: `/wave` writes measured `subagent-tokens:` and `orchestrator-tokens:` lines and a list-price `est-cost-usd:` estimate in the MR's `## Gates` section, and posts a Sonnet vs Opus `## Cost of this MR` comment; `/kaizen` reports cost by change class and by gate. One setting, `scripts/cost-ledger`, turns it on or off (on by default; a local opt-out is `scripts/cost-ledger off --local`). Ported from Blueprint; see `docs/development/cost-ledger.md` (#2128).
