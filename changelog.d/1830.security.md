Security: only a superuser can now grant all-content access (`can_access_all_content`), which reads and writes every board and group. A site admin who is not a superuser no longer sees **Grant all-content**, and `PATCH /api/v1/admin/users/{id}/` refuses the grant with `400`, on their own account and on any other; they can still revoke the flag. Every grant and revoke of `can_access_all_content` and `is_site_admin` is now recorded in the admin action log, with the actor (changes made by a management command have source `cli` and no actor). In the Django admin, accounts with all-content access now have the same protection as staff, superuser and site-admin accounts. An instance whose admins are not superusers should see the upgrade notes for how to restore the grant.
