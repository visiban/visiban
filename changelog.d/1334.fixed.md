`GET /api/boards/{id}/full/` now prefetches the board's `custom_field_definitions` and `swimlane_custom_field_definitions` instead of reading them with a live query each — closing a gap against the "every relation on `BoardFullSerializer` needs a matching prefetch" rule from #1212. Scoped to the `/full/` load only, so no other endpoint that shares `get_board_for_user()` pays for it.
