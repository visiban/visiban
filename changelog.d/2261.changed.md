The Analytics tab has one filter bar: its date range, swimlane and label drive every panel, including the dwell heatmap, the stalled cards and the blocked time. The heatmap's own 7 / 30 / 90-day buttons are removed with the 7-day window, so the heatmap now defaults to the last 90 days instead of 30. A bottleneck panel and four headline tiles (cycle time, throughput, work in progress, blocked time) open the page, followed by the charts in two-column rows.
