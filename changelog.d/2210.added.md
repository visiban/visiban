Parent cards on a board whose cards can have children show their children's progress on the card face ("4/7 done", a segment bar, the blocked count and when work last moved), computed for each viewer and refreshed live when a child moves, is archived or restored, or is blocked or unblocked. Children on a board you can't open show only as a count. The card API gains a per-viewer `children_rollup` field. (#2210)
