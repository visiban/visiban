Opening the Analytics overview no longer loads and replays the board's history once per chart. The page now gets its seven flow charts (cycle time, throughput, cumulative flow, WIP age, dwell, blocked time and needs attention) from one new request, `GET /api/v1/boards/{id}/metrics/overview/`, which loads the history once and returns each chart's numbers exactly as its own `metrics/*` endpoint does. Before, one open ran seven loads of the same history in the same server process at once. The single-chart endpoints are unchanged. The charts now load, and fail, together, while the dwell time heatmap keeps its own request.
