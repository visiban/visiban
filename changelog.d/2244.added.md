Boards and groups can ask for their health status to be reviewed every N days (1-365, off by default). Past that, the status shows "Not reviewed in N days" with a clock icon on the board and group pages, the group tree and the dashboard, until someone confirms or changes it. An inherited status follows the review setting of the group it comes from. The API adds `health_review_days` (writable) and `health_review_due_at` (read-only) to board and group payloads.
