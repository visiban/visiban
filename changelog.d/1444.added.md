Board admins can now invite someone to a board by email, even if they don't have an account yet. In **Board Settings → Members**, **Invite by email** sends a single-use invite that grants Member, Collaborator or Viewer and expires in 1, 7 or 30 days; **Pending invites** lists what was sent and lets any board admin revoke it. Redeeming an invite never lowers anyone's existing access — a group admin who follows a viewer invite stays an admin — and never adds the person to the board's group. On an invite-only site, an invite sent by a site admin also lets a new person create an account (password or OAuth); other admins' invites work for existing accounts only. Deactivating a user revokes the board invites they sent. New endpoints: `GET /api/v1/boards/{id}/invite-links/`, `POST /api/v1/boards/{id}/invite-links/send/`, `DELETE /api/v1/boards/{id}/invite-links/{link_id}/` and `GET`/`POST /api/v1/boards/join/{token}/`; the board WebSocket channel gains `invite_link.created` / `invite_link.revoked`, and `member.added` gains an `invite` key when the member joined through one. Registration with a multi-use site invite link now checks the one-redemption-per-address rule (#925) against the email saved on the new account rather than the raw request field.
