**Admin action log** — changes to instance-wide settings are now recorded with who made them and when, readable by site admins from `GET /api/v1/admin/action-log/`. Covers the maintenance mode toggle and its notice, registration mode, and the file-uploads switch, whether the change was made from the admin panel, the `maintenance_mode` management command, or the Django admin. Only real transitions are recorded, so the gap between an `enabled` and a `disabled` entry is the true length of a maintenance window. Previously the only trace of a flip was the setting's current value, which made "who put the instance into maintenance?" unanswerable during an incident retrospective.
