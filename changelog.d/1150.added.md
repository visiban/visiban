Add per-swimlane WIP and weight limits (#1150). A swimlane can set its own `wip_limit` and `weight_limit`, choose which columns count toward them (`limit_column_ids`, by default every column not marked done) and how they are enforced (`limit_enforcement`: `block`, `hard`, or `null` to follow the board's flags). Moves, card creates, restores and weight increases are checked on REST, MCP and the Django admin, with new `409` codes `swimlane_wip_limit_exceeded`, `swimlane_wip_hard_blocked` and `swimlane_weight_limit_exceeded`; a move between two counted columns of one swimlane never trips it, and the strictest tripped limit is reported. The Edit Swimlane modal sets the limits, the swimlane label shows Under / At / Over state, and a drag over a full cell shows a red ring and announces the verdict. JSON export and import round-trip the limits without enforcing them. The swimlane reorder endpoint now locks the board row `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`.
