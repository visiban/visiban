Swimlane bands can show a number rollup: choose one card number field in Board Settings → Rules → Swimlane rollup and each lane band shows its total over the lane's cards not in a done column, for example `$413k open`, updating live. The lane WIP line now reads `WIP 9 / 10` and, when over, `WIP 10 / 8 over` in warning text like the column header, replacing the red `⚠ Over WIP · 10/8` and its red accent line. API: new nullable, admin-writable `swimlane_rollup_field` on the board payloads and `/full/` (#2275).
