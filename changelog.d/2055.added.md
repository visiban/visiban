The webhook dispatcher now limits what one receiver gets, however many boards point webhooks at it: at most `WEBHOOK_HOST_MAX_PER_MINUTE` (default `120`) deliveries a minute and `WEBHOOK_HOST_MAX_CONCURRENCY` (default `2`) in flight at once per destination host, counted in the shared cache across dispatcher replicas (per process without one). A webhook whose host is at a limit waits for a later run; it is not a failure, so it neither backs off nor moves toward being turned off, and its events stay in order. `0` turns either limit off. New settings, Compose/env only (#2055).
