Board admins can now create shareable board invite links from Board Settings → Members (`POST /api/v1/boards/{id}/invite-links/`): a link grants member, collaborator or viewer access, always expires after 1, 7 or 30 days, can be single-use, and a board can have up to 5 active links. On an open site a newcomer can sign up from the link and join; on an invite-only or closed site a shareable link only admits people who already have an account. Site admins get a new **Board Invites** tab in the admin panel, backed by `GET/DELETE /api/v1/admin/board-invite-links/`, to list and revoke every board's invites. Board invite rows gain an additive `use_count` field.
