Clearing a pinned dropdown or checkbox on a card no longer makes it vanish from the card face. An unset pinned checkbox or dropdown now shows as a dashed ghost chip at every card density, and clicking it (or pressing Enter on it) sets the value again from the card. Before, at Comfortable and Standard density the field disappeared once cleared, while Board Settings still counted it toward the two-field pin limit, so it looked as if the field could not be re-pinned or replaced.
