Cross-board parent/child card links (API): a card on a board whose cards can have children can be linked as the parent of cards on one other board. New endpoints `POST`/`DELETE /api/v1/boards/{board_id}/cards/{id}/parent/`, `GET .../cards/{id}/children/` and `DELETE .../cards/{id}/children/{child_id}/`, two ids-only WebSocket events (`card.children_changed`, `card.parent_changed`), and link history on both cards. Linking needs member or higher on both boards; children on a board you cannot open are reported as a count only. The "Cards on this board can have children" setting can no longer be changed, through the API or the Django admin, while links depend on it. The board UI follows in a later release step.
