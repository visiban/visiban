Group invite links now track their creator's standing more closely (#1510). Deactivating a user with `PATCH /api/v1/admin/users/{id}/` deletes their personal access tokens and revokes their site, board and group invite links like the dedicated deactivate endpoint does. Demoting or removing a group admin revokes their unused links in that group and its subgroups, with an `invite_link.revoked` event per link, which also frees their slots under the active-link cap. The public join preview and join now check the creator first, so a link whose creator is gone is reported uniformly as `404` whatever the link's state (expired or consumed links with a live creator still answer `410`). The anonymous preview loads the group's ancestor chain in one query instead of one per level. Clearing `is_site_admin` or `can_access_all_content` through that endpoint revokes group links the user can no longer administer.
