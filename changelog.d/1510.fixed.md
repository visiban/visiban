Group invite links now track their creator's standing more closely (#1510). Deactivating a user with `PATCH /api/v1/admin/users/{id}/` revokes their site, board and group invite links like the dedicated deactivate endpoint does. Demoting or removing a group admin revokes their unused links in that group and its subgroups, with an `invite_link.revoked` event per link, which also frees their slots under the active-link cap. The public join preview and join now check the creator first, so a link whose creator is gone answers `404` whether it is also expired or consumed. The anonymous preview loads the group's ancestor chain in one query instead of one per level.
