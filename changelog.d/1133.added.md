Boards and groups can carry a health status: **On track**, **At risk** or **Off track**, each with its own shape and word as well as its color. A board admin sets a board's status from the pill beside the star on the board page, and a group admin sets a group's from the group page. A group's status carries down, live, to every subgroup and board below it (up to six levels) that does not set its own, and an inherited status is drawn dashed and names the group it comes from; a value set on a board or subgroup stays when a group above it changes. The status also shows on the group page's subgroup and board lists, the dashboard's board rows and the groups tree. It is set by hand only: there is no roll-up from boards to groups. A group's change updates that group's page live, while already-open pages of the boards and subgroups below it show the new inherited value after a reload. Additive API fields on every board and group payload and on the `board.created` / `board.updated` and `group.created` / `group.updated` events: `health_status` (`on_track` | `at_risk` | `off_track` | `null` to inherit; admin-writable), and read-only `effective_health_status` and `health_status_source` (`{id, name}` of the supplying group, or `null`). Migrations `boards/0095` and `groups/0024` add one nullable column each, with no index (#1133).
