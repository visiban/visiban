Muted text (captions, stats, placeholders, metadata) now meets WCAG AA contrast on every resting background in both themes. The dark muted color is brighter (it was 3.07:1 on cards and panels) and the light one is slightly darker (it was 4.34:1 on the page background). Empty-state messages, counts, timestamps, "(optional)" hints, the "+ Add card" button and always-visible remove and delete buttons no longer use the faintest gray, and chips and date fields on a gray fill use a stronger text color.
