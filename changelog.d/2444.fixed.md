The cumulative flow diagram and its `?date=` day drill-down no longer load every archived card's whole movement history into Python: the database picks the at most three rows per card (four for a drill-down day) that decide where the card rests, so Python receives those rows instead of every movement. Responses are byte-identical. On a board with three years of archived history, the drill-down's Python memory fell from about 150 MiB to about 43 MiB. Both requests still place every archived card, and on that board they are still not within the 1.5x of the load-test board that #2425 asked for (measured on PostgreSQL: latency about 6x for cumulative flow and about 14x for the drill-down).
