The cumulative flow diagram and its `?date=` day drill-down no longer read every archived card's whole movement history: each card's placement is now decided from the at most three rows (four for a drill-down day) it depends on, each found by a per-card index lookup on a new `card_movements` index (built concurrently). On a board with three years of archived history this cut the drill-down from about 2.1 s to 1.1 s and its peak memory from 150 MiB to 43 MiB, and cumulative flow from about 1.5 s to 1.1 s. Both still place every archived card, so their cost still grows with the number of archived cards. Responses are unchanged.
