With a cache-only session backend (`SESSION_ENGINE=django.contrib.sessions.backends.cache`), a brief cache outage no longer makes the WebSocket access re-check read every session as revoked. A frame that triggers the re-check during the outage now closes the socket with the reconnectable `4503` (#2142) instead of the final `4003`; an idle socket stays open and retries at its next ping. A missing or expired session still closes with `4003`. A cache outage at the handshake itself is handled separately (#2086).
