Boards set up before 1.3 now show the lane field tiers: the first two swimlane fields on the band and the rest behind **Show N more**, with Hidden on band, the counts and the **Manage swimlane fields** link (the **N fields** button and its **Change field order** link are gone). Nothing is hidden on the way: the lane details limit grows to the smallest choice that holds every field (up to 16), and a newly added field always gets a Show setting (#2658).
