Dropdown and multi-select custom fields can now give each choice a **color**, for both card and swimlane fields: pick one of eight palette colors (Slate, Blue, Green, Amber, Red, Violet, Pink, Teal) from the swatch in front of each choice in Board Settings, or **Reset to automatic**. A colored choice renders as a tinted badge, with its text label always shown, on the card face, in the swimlane label panel and in card detail, meeting WCAG AA contrast in light and dark themes; choices with no color keep their automatic dot, so existing boards look unchanged. Renaming or removing a choice resets its color. The API change is additive: `choice_colors`, a `{choice: palette_key}` map (default `{}`), on both custom field definition endpoints, their WebSocket events, and the JSON export's field schema (migration `0066`, a defaulted column); `choices` stays a plain list of strings. Keys that are no longer choices are pruned on save, an unknown palette key or a non-empty map on a type without choices is a `400`, and retyping a field away from dropdown/multi-select clears it (#1391).
