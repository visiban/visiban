`purge_card_history` now also redacts the purged cards' content in the board change feed (`GET /api/v1/boards/{id}/events/`). In `card.created`, `card.updated`, `card.unarchived` and `card.moved` events, the title, description, block reason, custom field values, external reference `ref` and `url`, and movement title and notes become `null`. Every payload key is kept. The same selector, dry run, audit entry and single transaction apply, and the action-log entry records `redacted_event_count`. Feed rows gain an additive `redacted_at` field. Before this, a purged card's title stayed readable to every board member through the change feed (#1816).
