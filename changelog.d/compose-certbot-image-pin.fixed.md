Pinned `certbot/certbot` to `v5.8.0` in `docker-compose.prod.yml`. It was the only unpinned image in the production stack — postgres, valkey and nginx all carried floors — and it is the service that owns TLS issuance and renewal, so it floated to whatever `latest` pointed at on every `docker compose pull`, with no diff and no pipeline event. `v5.8.0` is what `latest` resolved to at the time of the pin, so the change is a no-op today and a floor from here on.
