Group page: a board deleted or moved out of the group while the page was re-reading its boards list (after a reconnect, a late first connection or a health status change) no longer comes back, and a board created or moved in meanwhile no longer disappears. Before, the re-read replaced the whole list, so a deleted board stayed listed until the page was reloaded, since no later event removes it again; a rename or star change made meanwhile was also reverted until the next change to that board. Every board change that arrives during the re-read is now applied again on top of the server's answer, in the order it arrived (#2139).
