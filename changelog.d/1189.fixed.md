Converted `BoardFullSerializer.group_name` from a dotted-source `CharField(source="group.name", default=None)` to a `SerializerMethodField`, matching `BoardSerializer.group_name`'s #1166 fix. `BoardFullSerializer` is only ever instantiated read-only today (`/api/v1/boards/{id}/full/`), so this had no live effect, but the dotted-source form silently drops the field from the response whenever the serializer is bound with `partial=True` and the board has no group — this closes that trap before any future reuse can reintroduce it. Schema (`string, nullable, readOnly`) and the `/full/` response body are unchanged.
