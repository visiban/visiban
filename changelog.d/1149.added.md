Cards can be marked blocked with a reason and unblocked again (**Block…** in the card detail header, or `POST /api/v1/boards/{id}/cards/{id}/block/` and `.../unblock/`, and the new MCP `block_card` / `unblock_card` tools). Each block records who, when, why and the column the card was in, in a new `CardBlock` history (`GET .../cards/{id}/blocks/`) plus `blocked` / `unblocked` activity entries. The card face shows the existing blocked glyph with the elapsed time (`⊘ 3d`); the filter row gains **Blocked only**; Summary gains a per-swimlane **Blocked** count; Analytics adds blocked time per column and the top reasons. Additive API fields: `is_blocked` (manual or relation block), `blocked_at`, `blocked_reason` on cards and `card.updated` frames, `?blocked=` on the card list, `blocked_cards` on the summary, `blocked` on analytics, and `blocked_at` / `blocked_reason` / `blocks` per card in the JSON export (`schema_version` stays 2) (#1149).
