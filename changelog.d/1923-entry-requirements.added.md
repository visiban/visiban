Columns can now have entry requirements: conditions on a card's custom fields (filled, more than, at least, has at least N choices, dimension satisfied, or a whole field set complete) that a card must meet to move forward into the column, be created in it, or be restored into it. Each column chooses Warn (move and flag), Block (board admins, or an eligible assignee, can move it anyway with a reason) or Hard (no override). New API: `PUT /api/v1/boards/{id}/columns/{col_id}/entry-requirements/`, the `entry_requirements_unmet` 409 code, and the additive `entry_unmet` field on move, create and restore responses. Board export and import carry each column's requirements and mode by field and field set name; names that match nothing are skipped with a warning (#1923, #2582).
