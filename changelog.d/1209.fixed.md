- Fixed the OpenAPI schema (`/api/schema/`) misdescribing 4 endpoints that survived #1108's sweep: `GET .../cards/{id}/activities/` and `.../attachments/` previously fell back to the wrong component (`Card`) entirely, and `GET /api/notifications/` and `GET /api/share/{token}/` published no response body at all. All four now publish their real response shape, including correct nullability on `CardActivity.actor`, `CardAttachment.uploaded_by`, `Notification.actor`, `Notification.card_title`/`board_name`, and the public-share card's `assignee`. A client generated from the schema now gets accurate types for these endpoints. `scripts/check-serializer-ts-parity.py` now also checks these newly-published `CardActivity`, `CardAttachment`, `Notification`, and `PublicCard` schema components against their TypeScript interfaces.
