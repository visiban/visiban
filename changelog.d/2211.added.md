Swimlane default team: board admins can give a swimlane a default team in **Edit Swimlane**. New cards created in the lane, and new children added from its cards, are pre-assigned to the first team member who can be assigned on the new card's board; team members who can't access a child board are skipped. Removing someone from the board removes them from its lane teams. New `default_team` swimlane field and optional `apply_default_team` Add child field (#2211).
