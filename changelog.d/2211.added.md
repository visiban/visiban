Swimlane default team: board admins can give a swimlane a default team in **Edit Swimlane**. New cards created in the lane and children added with **Add child** from its cards are pre-assigned to the team's first eligible member (a card has one assignee; the others are named in the caption), shown as a removable chip. Edit Swimlane flags team members who can't be assigned on a child board the lane feeds and deactivated members, is a full-screen sheet on phones, and the lane band lists the team behind **Show N more**. Removing someone from the board removes them from its lane teams. New swimlane fields `default_team` and `default_team_members`, `GET .../swimlanes/{id}/default-team-access/`, `apply_default_team` on Add child, and `default_team` / `default_assignee_id` on `child-board-options` (#2211).
