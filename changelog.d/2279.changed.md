The redesigned board is now the only board. The `classic` preset id (in `Board.default_preset` and `?preset=` links) still works and shows the redesigned board; the Classic-only card face and its per-density snapshots are removed, and a screenshot baseline pins the redesigned board.

Dense board cards are now one calm row: they no longer show the description icon, the moved-N-days text, the recently-moved dot, the priority badge (shown for medium, high and urgent cards) or a priority-colored border. Priority stays in the card's accessible name and the hover peek, and labels, checklist progress, attachments, weight, due date and the reference link are read aloud by screen readers.
