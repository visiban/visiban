The OpenAPI schema now says how far constraint-shaped fields are enforced. `Column.wip_limit` and `weight_limit` and the board's `enforce_wip_limits`, `enforce_wip_hard` and `enforce_weight_limits` are described as binding on the card move endpoint only. `Board.allowed_priorities` and `Group.default_board_member_role` are marked as not yet enforced, `Group.allowed_priorities` as advisory, and `Card.version` as checked only on move. A new Field Enforcement architecture page classifies every such field as binding, advisory, or scaffold. Descriptions only — no field, type, or behavior changed (#1077).
