Board: split a column into 2 to 4 ordered sub-columns and show tracks as chips on cards. Board admins set them in the new Stage settings (column menu, or the Stage tab of column settings), including per-swimlane "not applicable" marks. Cards move between sub-columns by drag, by the card menu's Move on to / Move back to steps, or by picking a sub-column in Move to… (card menu, bulk toolbar, card details) and the card peek's Move (#2327). Leaving a stage before its last sub-column shows an announced warning from every way of moving a card and is recorded in the card's history; it is never blocked. At phone width a split stage gets a sub-column switcher with counts (#2330), and the card peek shows the current sub-column and each track's state (#2273). View → Show sub-columns switches the split view off (#2278, #1102).
