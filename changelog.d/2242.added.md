Health status now records who set it, when, what it was before and an optional note. The status tooltip and the group page say "Set 3d ago by Priya", the editor gains a note field, a **Confirm status** action that records a check without changing the status, and a **History** list of the last 20 changes; members and viewers can open it read-only. API: `health_status_changed_at`, `health_status_changed_by`, `health_status_note` on boards and groups, `changed_at` on `health_status_source`, and `POST /api/v1/boards|groups/{id}/health-status/` and `GET .../health-history/` (#2242).
