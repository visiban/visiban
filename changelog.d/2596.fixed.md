The board's Activity drawer no longer opens empty. Its **Recent** tab used to list only the board events that arrived while the board was open, so on a fresh load (and on every hosted-demo board, whose seeded cards all carry moves) it read "No activity yet". On first open it now loads the board's saved card moves and creations from the last 7 days (up to the drawer's 100 rows, with a note when a busy board has more), shows "Loading activity…" while it loads and a **Retry** line if the load fails, and adds live events on top. When the last 24 hours are empty but the week is not, it opens on the 7-day window instead of the 24-hour default, unless you have picked a window yourself. A live column move or card creation in the drawer is also credited to the person who made it, named the same way as in the saved rows, instead of "Someone".
