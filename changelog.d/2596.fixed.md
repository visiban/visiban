The board's Activity drawer no longer opens empty. Its **Recent** tab used to list only the board events that arrived while the board was open, so on a fresh load (and on every hosted-demo board, whose seeded cards all carry moves) it read "No activity yet". It now opens with the board's card moves and creations from the last 7 days and adds live events on top. A live column move in the drawer is also credited to the person who made it, instead of "Someone".
