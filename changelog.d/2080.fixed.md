Fixed: `get_accessible_group_ids` now lists every ancestor of a user's groups, not only the immediate parent, so members of deeply nested groups see the whole path in the sidebar (#2080).
A group's ancestors two or more levels up now also appear for navigation, and a member of both an ancestor and a deeper subgroup no longer loses sibling subgroups from their group list. Moving or importing a board into a visible grandparent group the caller does not administer now answers 403, as it already did for the parent, instead of 404.
