The analytics endpoint and the `aging_cards` MCP tool no longer build one board's dwell figures from a correlated per-card movement check, the same shape #2502 removed from the range metrics. The movement term is now an uncorrelated, board-bounded `IN`; the cards counted are unchanged. This is a partial improvement for #2506, not a complete fix. On the nightly fixture the two statements that read movements drop from 71,153 to 64,033 buffers for the load-test board (74,530 to 72,473 for the aged board), and the cards load and history read are faster in milliseconds (for the load-test board, 26.1 to 18.7 ms and 74.1 to 38.6 ms). The movements read was still instance-wide here, which #2567 narrows further on a board where most work is live (the aged board is unchanged); the history read still drives from a `card_movements_card_id` merge, and on the aged board the card filter still does a sequential scan of `cards`.
