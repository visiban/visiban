`DELETE /api/v1/auth/me/pending-email/` (cancel a pending email change) and `DELETE /api/v1/auth/me/pending-connect/` (dismiss the "Connect {Provider}?" prompt) now answer `204 No Content` with no body, like the other 1.2 revoke endpoints. In 1.2 pre-releases they answered `200` with the updated profile. Both endpoints are new in 1.2, so no 1.1 client is affected. A client that used the returned profile should re-fetch `GET /api/v1/auth/user/` instead; the bundled web app does (#2005)
