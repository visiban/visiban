Board and card panel: a card no longer briefly reverts to an older copy when the response to your own edit or move arrives after a WebSocket frame for a later change to the same card. The web app now compares the card's `version` and discards any copy older than the one on screen, for `card.updated`, `card.moved`, `card.created` and `card.unarchived` frames and for HTTP responses alike (#1969)
