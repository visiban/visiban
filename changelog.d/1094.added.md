Added a `/kaizen` skill that audits the development harness itself — it reads the `## Gates` ledger CLAUDE.md has required on every MR, computed via a new `scripts/kaizen_gate_ledger.py` parser, and reports each gate's run count, `n/a`/`skipped`/`0 findings` breakdown, yield, and ledger adoption rate across a window of recently merged MRs. It flags gates with fewer than 10 recorded runs as inconclusive, names concrete fast-path narrowing candidates for gates at 0% yield over 10+ runs (never a deletion — only a narrower trigger), and confirms gates over 50% yield as load-bearing. Distinct from `/pre-release`, which audits the codebase rather than the harness (#1094).
