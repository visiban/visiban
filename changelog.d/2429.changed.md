Analytics on very large boards loads less data: the Overview asks for at most 300 cards per list. On a test board with 12,000 cards in flight (uncompressed JSON), `metrics/overview` went from about 7.8 MB to about 0.58 MB and `analytics` from 1.9 MB to about 1.09 MB, so opening the Overview transfers about 1.67 MB instead of 9.75 MB; most of the remainder is the per-swimlane time-in-stage heatmap, which is not capped and grows with the number of swimlanes. Every headline number, percentile and count is still computed from every card; the cycle-time and WIP-aging charts plot a sample that always keeps the longest and oldest cards and say when they do. API: `metrics/cycle-time`, `throughput`, `wip-age`, `attention`, `overview` and `analytics` take a new optional `limit` (a positive integer, or `all`), which caps their per-card lists and adds `sampled` and the full counts (`samples_total`, `items_total`, `past_p85_count`, `stalled_cards_total`). Without `limit` every response is unchanged, and the other `metrics/*` endpoints ignore it as before.
