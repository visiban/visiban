Analytics on very large boards loads a fraction of the data: the Overview asks for at most 300 cards per list, so a board with 12,000 cards in flight transfers about a tenth of what it did (7.8 MB to under 0.6 MB for the flow charts). Every headline number, percentile and count is still computed from every card; the cycle-time and WIP-aging charts plot a sample that always keeps the longest and oldest cards and say when they do. API: `metrics/cycle-time`, `throughput`, `wip-age`, `attention`, `overview` and `analytics` take a new optional `limit` (a positive integer, or `all`), which caps their per-card lists and adds `sampled` and the full counts (`samples_total`, `items_total`, `past_p85_count`, `stalled_cards_total`). Without `limit` every response is unchanged.
