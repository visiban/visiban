A person a swimlane's default team pre-assigns to a new card (Add card in the lane, or Add child) now gets the usual "You were assigned to" notification, once, under their **Card assigned to me** preference. `POST .../cards/` takes an optional `notify_assignee` boolean for this (default `false`, so other creates with an assignee stay silent). Creating a card no longer also sends its assignee a "moved to" notification for the card's first column (#2211).
