A person a swimlane's default team pre-assigns to a new card (Add card in the lane, or Add child from its cards) now gets the usual "You were assigned to" notification, once, under their **Card assigned to me** preference, and not also a "moved to" notification for the card's first column. `POST .../cards/` takes an optional `notify_assignee` boolean for this (default `false`); every other create with an assignee, including the MCP `create_card` tool, behaves as before (#2211).
