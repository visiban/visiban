A child card's Parent block shows "2 of 5 siblings done". `Card.parent` gains `siblings_total` and `siblings_done`, computed for the viewer over the child's own board only (a blocked child is not done, as in the parent's rollup); a parent the viewer cannot open carries no sibling data. The line updates live: a write to one child sends its siblings an ids-only `card.parent_changed` with `siblings: true`, only to subscribers who can open the parent's board (it is not in the change feed or webhooks).
Toggling a child-board column's `is_done` refreshes the sibling lines through the board's own column event, not a `siblings` frame.
