Add the **Children** tab on cards of parent boards (#2209): a filtered view of the child board scoped to the card, with a done/blocked summary, the child board's own columns, counts and WIP limits, **Add child**, **Link existing**, per-child **Move** (through the child board's move endpoint and role) and **Unlink**, and keyboard navigation. The API adds `GET .../cards/{id}/child-candidates/` for Link existing, and the children read gains `position`, `assignee`, `blocked`, `blocked_at` and, when visible, the child board's columns and your role there.

The Children tab now re-reads when a child changes elsewhere (`card.children_changed`) and announces the change once to screen readers.

A child board's Filter panel gains a **Parent** row (Any, Any parent, No parent, or one parent you can open), with a chip, an `f_parent` URL parameter and saved-filter support (`state_json.parentFilter`, optional); the Children tab's **Open on <board>** link opens the child board filtered to that parent.
