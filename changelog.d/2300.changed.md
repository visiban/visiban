Board view: the column header row, every swimlane and every collapsed swimlane now share one column grid, so columns line up in every row. Columns share the board's width with 8 px between them, in proportion to their widths: an unresized board splits it equally, and dragging a column separator still resizes the column to its left, which now changes its share of the width while its edge follows the pointer. Below 1240 px of board width, or once a column would get narrower than 160 px (many columns, or one column widened far past the rest), the grid scrolls sideways inside its own box. A click on a separator still inserts a column (#2300).
