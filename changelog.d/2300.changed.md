Board view: the column header row, every swimlane and every collapsed swimlane now share one column grid, so columns line up in every row. Columns share the board's width with 8 px between them, in proportion to their widths: an unresized board splits it equally, and dragging a column separator still resizes the column to its left, which now changes its share of the width. Below 1240 px of board width (or about 160 px per column on a board with many columns) the grid scrolls sideways inside its own box. A click on a separator still inserts a column (#2300).
