Board: a full board refresh (on reconnect, tab focus, a version conflict or **Refresh board**) no longer briefly reverts a card change that arrived over the WebSocket while the refresh was in flight. For cards changed during the refresh, the copy with the higher `version` is kept, a card created or restored meanwhile stays, and one deleted or archived meanwhile does not come back. Every other card still takes the server's copy, so a refresh keeps repairing a board that missed events (#2038)
