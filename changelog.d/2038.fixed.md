Board: a full board refresh (on reconnect, tab focus, a version conflict or **Refresh board**) no longer briefly reverts a card change that arrived over the WebSocket while the refresh was in flight. For cards changed during the refresh, the copy with the higher `version` is kept, a card created or restored meanwhile stays, and a card deleted or archived on its own meanwhile does not come back. Every other card still takes the server's copy, so a refresh keeps repairing a board that missed events. A refresh can still briefly revert three kinds of change. The first is one that does not increment `version` (a comment, attachment, checklist item or relation), when the server read the board before it (#2089). The second is a card you are moving, while your move request is still in flight. The third is a column or swimlane deletion, which can bring back its cards too, or a label edit (#2130) (#2038)
