Board: a full board refresh (on reconnect, tab focus, a version conflict or **Refresh board**) no longer reverts a card change that arrived over the WebSocket while the refresh was in flight. For cards changed during the refresh, the copy with the higher `version` is kept, a card created or restored meanwhile stays, and a card deleted or archived on its own meanwhile does not come back. Every other card still takes the server's copy, so a refresh keeps repairing a board that missed events. Two kinds of change can still be reverted by a refresh. The first is a change that does not increment `version` (a comment, attachment, checklist item or relation), when the server read the board before it. It reverts until the next event for that card (#2089). The second is a card you are moving, which can briefly snap back while your move request is in flight (#2038)
