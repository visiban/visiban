Add experimental change-feed consumer clients for Python (`clients/python`, stdlib only) and TypeScript (`clients/typescript`, zero runtime dependencies): page `GET /boards/{id}/events/` with a pluggable cursor store and at-least-once delivery, a typed resync on `410 cursor_expired`, backoff on 429/5xx, and an experimental webhook signature helper. Not published to PyPI or npm; contract-tested against a live backend in CI (#1178).
