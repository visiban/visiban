Custom fields (#371) now have a full UI. Board admins can define, reorder, and pin up to 2 typed fields to the card face from a new **Fields** tab in Board Settings — text, number, date, dropdown, or checkbox. Pinned dropdown and checkbox values are editable directly from the card face without opening the card. Every field, pinned or not, is editable from the card detail panel, and the hover peek surfaces any non-pinned values that have data. The filter bar gained a control per pinned field, plus a picker to filter by any other custom field on demand.
