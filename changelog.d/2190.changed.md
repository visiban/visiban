The choices of a dropdown or multi-select custom field can now be reordered from the keyboard: each choice row's drag handle is a named button (Space to pick up, arrow keys to move, Space to drop, Escape to cancel without closing Board Settings).
The Board Settings card-field and swimlane-field lists now announce a drag by field name and position instead of an internal id, and Escape while dragging a field cancels the drag without also closing Board Settings.
