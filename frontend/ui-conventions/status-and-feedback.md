line per section of this file. A new section here also gets an index line there (#2681).
Sections here were moved verbatim from `frontend/CLAUDE.md` in #2681; inside them, "this file", "above" and "below" refer to the

## Badges and labels

- **Webhook status pill family (#863, a 1.3 UI spec Part 8 departure).** Active / Failing / Disabled, each with its own shape and a word: a filled circle, a triangle (with `· N failures`), a slashed ring. `px-2 py-0.5 text-xs font-semibold rounded-full border`, `/15` tint, `/60` border and on-tint text (`success` / `warning` / `fg-muted`, the #1550 ratios). The shape is an `aria-hidden` SVG; the word carries the meaning. Rendered only through `WebhookStatusPill`.

- **Priority pills use filled background everywhere** — blue/orange/red/dark-red fill (low/medium/high/urgent, `PRIORITY_COLORS` in `src/constants/colors.ts`). Text color comes from `readableTextOn(bg)` (near-black or white, whichever clears 4.5:1; #2466), the same on the board card, peek and card detail. Do not use outline/ring style; filled is more immediately scannable.
- **Text on a data-driven solid priority fill uses `readableTextOn(bg)`** (priority pills on the card, peek and card detail), never a hardcoded `#fff` or `text-on-primary`. Label chips stay on the tinted `colorChipStyle` pair, not `readableTextOn`. The check icon on a ringed swatch in `ChoiceColorPicker` is exempt: it is decorative and the ring carries the selected state. `text-on-primary` is otherwise only for token fills such as `bg-button-primary` (#2466).
- **Tinted non-pill priority controls (#1592, #1594)** — toggles such as `GroupDetail`'s "Allowed priorities" use the `/20` tint + on-tint text pattern (#1550): low=`info`, medium=`warning`, high=`danger`, urgent=`urgent` (`bg-urgent/20 border-urgent/60 text-urgent-on-tint`, dark red matching the board's `#B91C1C`). Urgent no longer needs a heavier border or `font-semibold` to differ from high. `--urgent-on-tint` contrast on the `/20` fill: 9.5:1 dark (11.3:1 on sunken), 7.1:1 light (6.8:1 on sunken). Keep the label text and expose state with `aria-pressed`.
- **`stale-severe` is neither `urgent` nor `danger` (#1841)** — it is reserved for the ≥3× card aging stage (§ Card aging ramp). Do not reuse it for priority or error states, and do not use `urgent` for aging.
- **`stale-tint` is reserved for the 1-2× card aging stage fill (`bg-stale-tint/15`, #2029).** It is pinned to the pre-#2029 orange (orange-600 light / orange-400 dark), independent of `--warning`, so the ramp's measured ratios do not move when the warning text token is retuned. Never use it for generic warning surfaces (those use `bg-warning/N`), and do not retune it alongside `--warning`.
- **`urgent` is not an alias of `danger` (#1594)** — `--urgent` is a fixed red-700 in both themes (matches `PRIORITY_COLORS.urgent`, `#B91C1C`); `--urgent-on-tint` flips per theme. Use it only for the urgent priority level; generic error and destructive states stay `danger`. Priority colors come from `PRIORITY_COLORS` (filled pills) or the `info`/`warning`/`danger`/`urgent` tokens (tinted controls); never hardcode per-priority hex maps.
- **Label pills are an exception to the filled-pill rule** — labels carry arbitrary user-assigned colors (any `#RRGGBB`, including near-white and near-black), so white-on-fill would fail contrast on light labels and the raw color fails as text over its own tint. Never write `color + "22"` (it also breaks on non-6-digit values). Render every label chip through `colorChipStyle(color)` (`src/utils/colorContrast.ts`) with the `cf-choice-badge` class plus `border`: the bg is the `choiceColors.ts` recipe (14% over white, 22% over `#1E293B`), the fg walks from the color toward black (light) or white (dark) until it reaches 6:1 against that bg (asserted in `colorContrast.test.ts` on a 6x6x6 sample of the RGB cube plus the extremes, not exhaustively; the algorithm ends at pure black or white, which always clears 6:1), and a non-hex legacy value gets the neutral slate pair. The `--cf-*` custom properties switch with `data-theme`, so there is no re-render and no `dark:` duplicate. A toggle chip (`CardDetail` label picker) shows its active state with a check mark (rendered only when active, in a fixed `w-3` box with `gap-1`; the idle chip holds that 1rem as symmetric padding instead, `px-[1.125rem]` against the active `px-2.5`, so the idle name is centered, the active check and name are centered as a group and toggling keeps the width, #2618; never reserve the room with an `invisible` check, which pushes the idle name off center; the `font-semibold` weight can still widen the chip by about 1-2px), `font-semibold`, an inset `currentColor` ring and `aria-pressed`, never a fill change alone; do not dim an idle chip with `opacity`, which drops it below 6:1. **A chip under an ancestor's `opacity` passes its dimmed surface (#2102):** `colorChipStyle(color, dim)` with `dim` one of `DIMMED_CHIP_SURFACES` (`warning`, `stale`, `stale2x`, `stale3x` on the card face, which passes `aging.stage`; `lens-closed` on the closed-issue `LensIssueCard`, `opacity-70` over the lane's `bg-canvas`). The bg (the hue) is unchanged; the fg walks further toward black/white until the pair holds 6:1 *as rendered*, after the opacity blends both toward the backdrop. Pinned floors over the sampled cube, the samples and the legacy neutral pair (`colorContrast.test.ts`): 6:1 on every light surface and the dark warning stage; dark stale / 2x / 3x 5.91 / 5.91 / 5.92 and dark closed Lens 5.08, where pure white is the most the fg can do. Before, they fell to about 4.3 light on the stale stages and 3.1 light / 3.6 dark on the closed Lens card. The opacity stays (it is the stage and closed-state cue); a new dimmed surface gets a `DIMMED_CHIP_SURFACES` entry, whose backdrop `onTintTokens.test.ts` recomputes from the tokens and stage classes. This is one of two permitted tinted, bordered pills in the system — see the custom-field chip exception below for the other.
- **Color dots carry a constant 1px neutral ring (#2094)** — a user-chosen dot can be near-white (1.0:1 on the light surface) or near-black (1.44:1 on dark). Put the `color-dot` class (`index.css`, a mid-gray `box-shadow` ring, so no Tailwind `ring`/`shadow` utility on the same element) on every dot filled from a user or board color: column, lane and group-label dots, filter-chip and dropdown dots. Color stripes beside a name get no treatment; they are decorative.
- **Pinned custom-field chips are a second, distinct exception to the filled-pill rule** — the card-face custom-field chip (`CustomFieldValueDisplay`, `CardItem`) uses `border border-line rounded` with plain `text-fg-secondary` value text; the chip itself is never filled or tinted — the one tint permitted is a colored choice's badge *inside* it (see the colored-choices rule below). Custom fields need their own visual language distinct from both the priority pill (filled, semantic urgency color) and the label pill (tinted, user-assigned color) — collapsing all three into one visual bucket would make it impossible to scan a card face and tell "this is a priority" from "this is a label" from "this is a custom field" at a glance. The neutral bordered chip signals "structured metadata, no inherent color" the way a table cell border does, leaving the field's own semantics (a dropdown choice's color dot or color badge, a date, a number) to carry any further meaning inside it.
- **Colored dropdown and multi-select choices may render as a tinted badge (#1391).** When a field choice has an explicit color, its value renders as a tinted badge inside the neutral bordered chip on the card face and in the row header: `rounded px-1.5 py-0.5 text-xs`, with fg and bg from the 8-key `{light, dark}` palette in `src/constants/choiceColors.ts`. Rules: (1) The text label is always rendered. Color never carries meaning alone, and the badge is never an icon or dot only. (2) Every key must reach at least 4.5:1 against its badge background in both themes (currently at least 6.0), and any new key requires a recorded ratio. (3) A choice with no explicit color keeps the existing neutral chip and hash `choiceColor` dot, so existing boards look unchanged. (4) An orphaned value (choice removed or renamed) renders neutral with no color. (5) Never use the tinted badge for the field name or a non-choice type. It is the second permitted tinted pill, after label pills (priority pills are filled, not tinted), and it remains distinct from a label because it sits inside the field chip. **Mechanics:** render it only through `ChoiceBadge` (`components/Card/ChoiceValue.tsx`, which also owns the one `ChoiceDot`), resolve the key only through `explicitChoiceColor()` (`utils/customFieldValue.ts` — it returns `null` for unset, orphaned and unknown keys), and never inline the palette hex: `choiceBadgeStyle()` passes the pair as `--cf-{bg,fg}-{light,dark}` custom properties and the `cf-choice-badge` class in `index.css` picks the pair for the active `data-theme`, so no `dark:` duplicate is needed. An explicit color *replaces* the dropdown dot; it never sits beside one. On the card face a quick-editable badge carries the edit hint as an inner text span with `underline decoration-dotted underline-offset-2` — the decoration is `currentColor`, so it always matches the badge fg's contrast — never as a `border-b` on the badge, which would change its height. In card detail (`variant="detail"`) the value renders as a bare `ChoiceBadge` with no bordered chip around it, the same way detail renders every other value as plain text rather than a chip.
- **(Superseded by § Swimlane bands, #2271: the row chip is now `variant="band"`, plain "Label value" text; the rules below apply only to what is still shared, the padlock and value formatting.)** **Swimlane-row custom-field chips are the same neutral bordered chip, with two deviations the surface forces (#1140)** — the row chip is `CustomFieldValueDisplay` at `variant="row-chip"` and keeps `border border-line rounded` with a `text-fg-tertiary` name (unless hidden by the board setting, #1418; `fg-muted` until #1862) and `text-fg-secondary` value, so a custom field reads as a custom field whether it hangs off a card or a row. It is **not** a fourth exception to the filled-pill rule. What differs: (a) `max-w-full min-w-0` replaces `max-w-[10rem]` and the value truncates at 20 characters instead of 16, because the swimlane label panel was a `sidebarWidth ?? 220`px *vertical* column, not a horizontal metadata row — row chips stack, so each owns a full line and a 160px cap would truncate for nothing; (b) an `is_admin_only` definition renders a leading `w-3 h-3 text-fg-tertiary` padlock **inline SVG on `currentColor`** (`role="img"`, `aria-label="Admin-only field"`) before the dropdown color dot, and appends ` · Only board admins can see this` to the chip's `title`. Never a `🔒` emoji — an emoji paints in its own fixed color and stops tracking the theme. Only an admin ever receives an admin-only value, so the padlock never renders for a member; it exists so an admin can tell, without opening Board Settings, which values on their screen their team cannot see. The same padlock marks the same fact in the Board Settings → Swimlane fields list — one glyph, one meaning, both surfaces.
- **Row-chip field names are a board-level admin setting, not a per-viewer preference (#1418).** `Board.show_row_chip_field_names` (default on) is one switch for the whole board, at the top of Board Settings → Swimlane fields; non-admins get a read-only line. It is threaded `BoardView` → `SwimlaneRow` (`hideRowChipFieldNames`) → `CustomFieldValueDisplay` (`hideName`), and `computeAutoSwimlaneWidth` takes the same flag so the label column does not stay sized for names it no longer shows. It affects `variant="row-chip"` only: card-face chips always show the name, and the lane field grid (`LaneFieldGrid`, #2354) always lists name and value. Do not add a per-field or per-viewer override on top of it.
- **A hidden-name row chip keeps its `title` and carries the name for assistive tech in an sr-only span (#1418).** With `hideName`, the visible `text-fg-tertiary` `{name}:` span is replaced by `<span className="sr-only">{name}: </span>` — never `aria-label` on the chip `<span>`, which ARIA 1.2 prohibits on a generic role. The `title` stays `{name}: {value}` (plus the admin-only suffix), and everything else on the chip is unchanged: padlock, hash dot, `ChoiceBadge`, multi-select sub-chips, empty-value omission, the 20-character cap. The value simply takes the freed width.
- **Checkbox-type fields never hide their name (#1418).** A bare `Yes`/`No` is meaningless without its field, so `hideName` is ignored when `field_type === "checkbox"`, both in `CustomFieldValueDisplay` and in the auto-width estimate.
- **Hiding a visible label must not remove the only touch/keyboard path to it (#1418).** When a display setting hides text that a hover `title` otherwise supplies, either keep a focusable trigger that reveals it (for example the lane details) or the helper text must not promise one. The lane "Show N more" toggle is that trigger; it shows on every lane of a board with more than two eligible fields (the older "All fields" trigger was retired by #2303/#2354).
- **An interactive control's visible text must be contained in its accessible name (WCAG 2.5.3 label-in-name) (#1509).** When a button has both visible text and an `aria-label`, the `aria-label` starts with the visible text, for example `All fields: Show all 3 field values for Acme`. Voice-control users say what they see.
- **Compact chip-style triggers keep a minimum 24px hit area (`min-h-6`) (WCAG 2.5.8) (#1509).** Small `text-xs` pill triggers such as the swimlane row "Show N more" button carry `min-h-6` so the target is at least 24px tall.
- **A multi-select value is neutral sub-chips inside the same neutral bordered chip (#1391).** The value cell holds `MultiSelectChips`: `text-xs px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded truncate bg-surface-hover text-fg-secondary`, each with its full text in `title` — never color-dotted. An entry whose choice has an explicit color is a tinted `ChoiceBadge` with the same width cap instead (see the colored-choices rule above); pass the field's `definition` to `MultiSelectChips` so it can resolve them. Card face: at most **2** sub-chips (each `max-w-[6rem]`) then a `+N` (`text-fg-tertiary text-xs`, #1862) whose `title` lists every entry; the outer chip is `max-w-[14rem] min-w-[4rem]` (not `shrink-0`, because the card metadata row is single-line `overflow-hidden`) and the field name (`min-w-0 shrink-[3]`) truncates before the value chips do. Swimlane row header: at most **3**, wrapping (`flex-wrap gap-1`, sub-chips `max-w-full`). Detail and the editor trigger show all, wrapping. The card-face chip is read-only for this type — no quick-edit role and no dotted underline; edit in card detail.
- **One shared admin-only padlock component — never a second hand-rolled copy (#1140).** The `is_admin_only` padlock (`w-3 h-3 text-fg-tertiary` inline SVG; `fg-faint` until #1862, which is 1.93:1 dark and below the 3:1 a meaningful glyph needs; `role="img"`, `aria-label="Admin-only field"`) is rendered from `components/Common/AdminOnlyFieldGlyph.tsx` and imported everywhere it appears — the row chip, `SwimlaneFieldEditRow`, `SwimlaneFieldsPopover`, and the Board Settings → Swimlane fields list. Do not re-inline the `<rect>`/`<path>` markup at a new call site. This is the same drift the codebase already guards against for `toolbarIcons.tsx` and `relativeTime()`, and it had already happened here: the glyph shipped as four inline copies and one of them announced `aria-label="Admin only"` while the other three said `"Admin-only field"`, silently splitting the accessible name across surfaces the rule above requires to say one thing.
- **Status pill tone map.** Invite status pills (pending/used/expired/revoked) come only from `INVITE_STATUS_STYLES` in `src/constants/invites.ts`. Do not define a local status-to-class map. A danger status uses `bg-danger/20 text-danger-on-tint`, never `bg-danger/60` or another high-alpha fill behind same-hue text (about 2:1 contrast) (#1501). The text half of every status tint comes from the on-tint tokens, not `text-danger`: see **On-tint status text** below (#1550).
- **Health status pills (#1133).** Board and group health (On track / At risk / Off track / No status) renders only through `HealthStatusPill` / `HealthListingPill` (`components/Common/HealthStatusPill.tsx`), with every class, word and description from `src/constants/healthStatus.ts` (`HEALTH_STATUS_STYLES`, `HEALTH_STATUS_LABELS`, `HEALTH_STATUS_DESCRIPTIONS`). Never define a local health-to-class map. Rules: (1) tint + shape + word, always: `bg-<hue>/20 border-<hue>/60 text-<hue>-on-tint` with success / warning / danger, and `bg-fg-muted/20 border-fg-muted/60 text-muted-on-tint` for No status; the four pairings are pinned at >= 5.00:1 on canvas, sunken and surface in both themes (5.26 before #2029 darkened the light `/20` fills) in `src/test/onTintTokens.test.ts`. (2) An **inherited** value (the entity sets nothing; `health_status_source` names the group) is the same pill with `border-dashed`, an sr-only `, inherited from {Group}` suffix and a `title` naming the group; a value explicitly set equal to the parent's is "set here" and solid. (3) **Listings** (board rows, subgroup cards, the group tree, the dashboard) show a read-only `<span>` pill only when `effective_health_status` is non-null, for everyone, and never on a `navigation_only` group; No status is never listed. (4) **Headers** (board Row 1, group meta line) use `HealthStatusControl` (the board Row 1 passes `showUnsetToReaders`, #2450: the reference shows the hollow No status pill to every viewer there, so a non-admin sees it as a read-only pill; other headers keep the next rule): an admin gets a `<button>` trigger, including the hollow No status pill as the place to set one; a non-admin gets a trigger that opens the popover **read-only** (header, set line, note, History auto-expanded, one **Close** button; #2242), or nothing when no status applies — never a greyed-out control. A plain non-admin pill remains only in `mode="basic"` (every lane) or without `fetchHistory`. (5) The accessible content is real text (`Health status: ` sr-only prefix + word), never an `aria-label`, so a row button's name reads "{name} Health status: At risk" and the visible word is always in the name. Header pills may pass `collapseLabel` (word `sr-only md:not-sr-only`, shape only below `md`). The swimlane band (#2243, #2271) is also a header under rule (4), with one recorded exception: on a repeated-row surface the admin's unset affordance is a hover/focus-revealed icon button (visible at rest on coarse pointers via `[@media(hover:none)]:opacity-100`), not a No status pill; a set value is always visible, and non-admins see nothing when unset.
- **Health review cue (#2244).** "Not reviewed in N days" renders only through `HealthReviewCueBadge` (`components/Common/HealthStatusPill.tsx`), built only by `healthReviewCue` (`utils/healthStatus.ts`) from the server's `health_review_due_at`. It is a 12px `aria-hidden` clock plus words in `text-warning` with no fill: never color alone, and never an on-tint token (it sits on the solid surface, where `text-warning` clears AA per the #2029 note above). It sits beside the pill in listings (`HealthListingPill`; `compactCue` in the narrow group tree shows `18d` at every width) and in board/group headers (`HealthStatusControl`; with `collapseLabel` the short `18d` form shows only below `md`, like the pill's word), never on swimlanes. The visible `18d` is `aria-hidden`; the full sentence is always in the accessible text after an sr-only `, `, so a row button's name reads "{name} Health status: At risk, Not reviewed in 18 days". Its `title` only supplements (unreachable on touch and keyboard); the full sentence and the hint are also text in the health popover (`reviewCue` prop on `HealthStatusPopover`, #2310), the touch-reachable surface, opened from the board and group header status button (the group tree row has no popover; its compact `18d` stays tooltip-only and a tap navigates to the group page): words only, no clock, `text-xs font-medium text-warning` after the set line, with the hint in `font-normal text-fg-tertiary` and only where Confirm shows; never in a lane popover. The "Confirm or change the status to clear this" hint appears only on an admin's header control (`actionable`), never on listings or for non-admins. **Touch decision (#2322):** the group tree row's compact "18d" cue gets no touch-only explanation of its own. The row is a navigation button (a nested popover or long-press would conflict with it), the full sentence is in the row's accessible name, and a tap lands on the group page, whose header status opens the popover with the sentence and the "Confirm or change" hint. Revisit only if real touch users report the bare "18d" as unclear.
- **Lane health status (#2243).** Renders only through `HealthStatusControl entityKind="swimlane"` (fields from `laneHealthFields()` in `utils/healthStatus.ts`, so `inheritedFrom` is always null): solid, never dashed, because a lane never inherits from the board and never rolls up to it. Expanded lane: the set pill sits inline after the name in the band (`scope="lane"`, `collapseLabel`); collapsed lane: a shape-only inline pill (`shapeOnly`) beside the name; the band keeps the same height with or without a status (guarded by `e2e/swimlane.spec.ts`), and nothing when unset, even for admins. The unset trigger (`trigger="icon"`) is the first button of the right-hand cluster, admins only, expanded only. The popover has four choices (On track / At risk / Off track / No status), no Inherit, no note and no history, plus the line "This lane's status is separate from the board's status."; board/group extras added to `HealthStatusPopover` must stay off for `entityKind="swimlane"` until lanes get history: both `HealthStatusControl` and `HealthStatusPopover` force `mode="basic"` for that kind, and `SwimlaneRow` also passes `mode="basic"` explicitly (#2242). The pill's sr-only prefix names the lane (`Health status for lane {name}: `). Clicks and double-clicks on the pill, the icon and the portaled popover never reach the band's double-click edit shortcut (wrap in `onDoubleClick={e => e.stopPropagation()}`). A save that swaps triggers hands focus explicitly (set: to the new pill; clear: to the icon, or to the lane name on a collapsed lane, which has no icon) via `autoFocus`; never leave a pending focus request for an element that is not rendered, or it fires on a later mount. An open popover survives remote updates: the trigger whose popover is open stays mounted (`onOpenChange`) until it closes, even if the value flips set <-> unset. The unset icon keeps a 24px hit target (`min-w-6 min-h-6`) around its 16px glyph. The announcement goes through the row's own live region (`onAnnounce`) because the saving control may unmount. Escape stays at tier 25.
- **Health status attribution and history (#2242).** (1) The "Set 3d ago by Priya" copy comes only from `healthSetLine` (`utils/healthStatus.ts`; tooltip via `healthTitle`, group caption via `healthCaption`); never build it inline. Time is `formatRelativeTime` (no new formatter): relative within 7 days, "on Sep 14" after; wherever a time renders it is `<time dateTime={iso} title={formatDateTimeUser(iso, prefs)}>`. A null `changed_by` renders **System** (with the title "Automatic change or removed account"); a null `changed_at` omits the clause. (2) A reconfirm is the explicit **Confirm status** action only (`POST .../health-status/`); **Save** never records a no-change, and Enter on an unchanged radio closes with no request. Enter in the note with an unchanged draft moves focus to Confirm, never confirms. (3) `HealthStatusPopover` / `HealthStatusControl` take `mode`: `"detailed"` (default: set line, note, Confirm, History) and `"basic"` (exactly the #1133 editor), forced for `entityKind="swimlane"` (see Lane health status above). (4) History is bounded: the last 20, a static "Showing the 20 most recent changes." line when `has_more`, never load-more or a cursor. (5) The non-admin header pill opens a read-only popover (amends health-pill rule (4) above). (6) The note is saved only with explicit **Save** or **Confirm status** and discarded on Cancel, Escape or outside click; it always starts empty and is one line (newlines become spaces, Shift+Enter does nothing). Disabled-button reasons are sr-only `aria-describedby` spans placed outside the button so they stay out of its name.
- **Auto health status (#2245).** (1) Status-origin glyphs (`PinGlyph` set by hand, `AutoGlyph` calculated, in `HealthStatusPill.tsx`) are `aria-hidden` and draw in `currentColor` with no color class of their own, so they take the pill's on-tint text color and its measured contrast; they are never the only signal: the origin is also in the pill's accessible text and the popover's Calculated / Set by hand lines. (2) A control disabled because a setting is off (the Off track threshold while auto health is off) dims only its own row (`opacity-60` on the input row); the hint that says why it is disabled, and every other help line, stays full opacity so it keeps its contrast. (3) A `ToggleField` with a `description` passes the same id as `descriptionId` and `aria-describedby`, so the switch's accessible description is the visible text (see the density override and the auto health toggles in `BoardSettingsModal`). (4) A settings toggle or threshold that saves on change ignores a second commit while its PATCH is in flight (a ref guard, plus `ariaDisabled` on the toggle), and a save on Enter is not repeated by the blur that follows. (5) The Recompute now button's feedback slot is always rendered (`min-h-4`) so an error or the visible "Recomputed" cue never shifts the layout; the polite live region carries the announcement.
- **On-tint status text (#1550).** Text on a `bg-<hue>/20`..`/30` status tint uses `text-danger-on-tint`, `text-warning-on-tint`, `text-success-on-tint`, `text-info-on-tint`, or `text-muted-on-tint` (for `bg-fg-muted/20`), never the plain `text-<hue>` token, which is tuned for text on a solid surface and falls below 4.5:1 on its own tint. Never put a `/40`..`/60` fill behind same-hue text; selected states use `bg-<hue>/20` with the on-tint text and a stronger `border-<hue>/60`. Measured WCAG ratios (token RGB composited over the surface; worst case across canvas, sunken and surface):

  | Pairing on `/20` tint | Before dark | After dark | Before light | After light |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | danger | 3.93 | 5.73 | 3.24 | 5.58 |
  | warning | 4.54 | 6.09 | 2.56 | 5.26 (5.01 since #2029) |
  | success | 5.36 | 6.65 | 2.43 | 5.27 (5.00 since #2029) |
  | info | 4.03 | 5.68 | 3.59 | 6.06 |
  | muted (revoked) | 2.49 | 7.97 | 3.42 | 7.44 |

  Heatmap/strip `/30` fills (danger, warning, success, info) measure at least 4.69:1 dark and 4.75:1 light with the on-tint text; `/40` and above fail (danger `/40` is 4.03 dark). Since #2030 the `used` invite pill is `bg-fg-muted/20 text-muted-on-tint` (`fg-tertiary` on the brighter dark fill fell to 4.20), the same tone as `revoked`, so the revoked pill's `line-through` and the word are what separate them. Any new on-tint token needs a recorded ratio here (`success-on-aging-tint`, used only through `card-aging-meta`, is recorded in § Card aging ramp). The same text applies on the lighter `/10` and `/15` tints (banners, toggles, connection chips, overdue chips): plain `text-danger` on `bg-danger/10` is 3.79:1 in light mode, `text-warning` and `text-success` are 2.7 to 3.2:1, whereas on-tint text measures at least 5.56:1 on `/10`..`/15` in both themes (dark 6.22 or more). A stronger armed or `isOver` fill (for example the column trash zone's `bg-danger/50`) keeps the fill and border as the signal and sets the label to `text-fg` (5.17:1 dark, 7.33:1 light, worst surface); never reach for an on-tint token above `/30`. Remaining `/10`..`/15` hits with no same-hue text are intentional: the aging overlay, the swimlane search pulse, and the selected history row, which carry no text of their own. A `hover:bg-<hue>/30` on a bordered action uses `hover:bg-<hue>/20 hover:text-<hue>-on-tint`.
- Filter active-count badge: `bg-primary-emphasis/20 text-info` — always use the `primary-emphasis` token for the fill so the badge tracks the active theme
- Consistent badge sizing: `px-2 py-0.5 text-xs rounded-full`
- Neutral informational identity chip (admin Users table **Superuser**, #2083): `px-1.5 py-0.5 text-xs rounded-full bg-surface-hover text-fg-secondary`. It does not use the `primary-emphasis` fill, which is the **Admin** chip's fill, so the two read as distinct facts in the same cell.
- When an `OverflowMenu` is shortened by a visible reason line, give the line an `id` and pass it as `triggerDescribedBy` so the trigger is described by it (#2083).
- Active filter chip (`FilterChip.tsx`, the dismissible tag shown per applied filter in the filter row): `bg-primary-emphasis/20 text-info` fill with a `border border-primary-emphasis/40` border — same fill/text token pair as the badges above, plus the border so it reads as removable rather than a plain count/state indicator (#1239)
- **Tagged filter chips (#2205).** `FilterChip`'s optional `tag` prop renders a leading `px-1.5 rounded-full bg-surface-hover text-fg-secondary text-xs` pill naming the filter family (lane chips use `tag="Lanes"`), and a tagged chip is named `{tag}: {label}, remove`. Untagged chips keep `Remove {label} filter`; do not migrate them piecemeal. Between lane chips a plain `<span className="text-xs text-fg-tertiary">and</span>` / `or` (not `aria-hidden`) carries the join. Dismissing a lane chip moves focus to the chip that takes its place, else the **Lane filters** trigger.
- **`bg-info/*` vs `bg-primary-emphasis/*` — know which one your state is.** These are not interchangeable, and `--info`/`--primary` are different values in dark mode (they only coincide in light mode, which is why this drifts invisibly). Two patterns, never cross them:
  - **Transient disclosure / toggle-open state** (a button whose job is "is this popover/menu/panel currently open" — Filters button, kebab trigger, SplitButton chevron, dropdown trigger's open state): `text-info bg-info/10`.
  - **Persistent selection / "where you are" / "what's active" state** (nav-active item, tree-active item, flyout-active item, filter active-count badge, active filter chip, radio selected, grid-overlay cell tint, lens "Current" pill, mode-indicator banners use `bg-primary/15` instead): `bg-primary-emphasis/*` (paired text token varies by context — `text-info`, `text-warning`, etc. — keep whatever paired text token the surface already uses; only the background fill is the `primary-emphasis`-vs-`info` decision).

  When introducing or touching any new "this item is the active/selected/current one" treatment, grep for `bg-info` in the file first and ask which bucket above it falls into before picking a token. Prior incidents: #1002, #1239 (`SavedFiltersDropdown`/`AdminPage`), #1336 (`AppSidebar`/`CollapsedFlyout`).

## Truncation inside nested flex chips

Any chip built as an `inline-flex` container around one or more `truncate` text spans (custom-field chips, multi-select sub-chips, and any future nested-flex chip) must give **every** truncating span an explicit `min-w-0` (or a numeric `min-w-[Nrem]` floor), and give the **chip itself** `overflow-hidden`. A flex child's default `min-width: auto` is content-based — without an explicit override, `truncate`'s ellipsis never engages inside a flex container, and long content silently overflows the chip's own box onto whatever renders next in the row (#1411: this is why pinned custom-field chips rendered on top of their neighbors instead of clipping). A chip that needs to shrink in its own parent flex context (e.g. the card metadata row) must not be `shrink-0` with only a `max-w` — pair an explicit `min-w-[Nrem]` floor with the `max-w` instead, or it will overflow its parent rather than shrink into it.

`overflow-hidden` on a chip is a backstop, not the truncation mechanism: every flex child that can hold long text still needs `min-w-0` plus `truncate`. Anything inside a clipped chip with a focus ring needs at least ring-width (2px) of padding on each clipped edge, so do not reduce the chip's `py-0.5`.

## Decorative glyphs (#2012)

- **A decorative glyph rendered in its own `<span>`/element is `aria-hidden="true"`.** This covers separators and hover affordances that sit beside the real content: the hover "+" (`SeparatorPlus`, used by `RowSeparator`, `ColumnSeparator` and `SwimlaneRow`), breadcrumb dividers (`/`, `›`), the `·` between notices, and the sidebar's split "+" spans next to "New board" / "New group". Otherwise a screen reader announces a stray "+" or "slash".
- **Inline "+ Label" text is out of scope.** When the "+" is part of the same text node as the label (e.g. `+ New board`), it is read as part of the label; do not split it out just to hide it.
- **A glyph that is the only content of a control needs an `aria-label` on the control, not `aria-hidden`** — hiding it would leave the control unnamed.
- A bare `eslint-disable` also needs a `-- reason` (see `docs/development/suppressions.md`).

## Security notices

- **Security notices ignore email opt-in (#863, a 1.3 UI spec Part 8 departure).** A webhook disable notice (`webhook_disabled`) is emailed even when the recipient turned notification email off, and its footer says so. It carries exactly three facts: board name, subscription id and reason code (with the plain label derived from the code), never the URL, the host or anything the receiver sent back. In-app it reads as one notification whose hint is "Open board settings →", linking to `/boards/{id}?settings=webhooks` with no subscription id; BoardView opens the Webhooks tab for an admin and strips the parameter.

## Inline status messages

- **Address refusals share one message (#863, Part 8 departure 26).** Any outbound-target refusal that depends on where an address points (a private range, loopback, cloud metadata, or a name that resolves to one) uses one neutral message that names the operator: "Visiban can't send to this address. If it should be allowed, ask your Visiban operator." (`ADDRESS_REFUSED_COPY`). Separate copy would let a user map the internal network. Only input-shape errors (scheme, format, embedded credentials) get their own copy, and the client checks only those; the test event's "not sent" result reuses the same message.

- **Reserve vertical space unconditionally** — render the container element always (`<p className="text-xs h-4">`) and conditionally render the text content inside it. Never conditionally render the container itself; doing so causes buttons and surrounding elements to shift when messages appear or disappear.
- Error text: `text-danger`; success text: `text-success` — always on a `<span>` inside the reserved container, not directly on the `<p>`
- **Relabel destructive-escape actions after success** — if a modal stays open after a successful action, relabel "Cancel" to "Close" once success state is set, so the button's semantics match the user's situation
- **`h-4` reserves exactly one line — size the reservation to the copy.** A slot whose longest state wraps to two lines uses `h-8`; a slot holding a full sentence whose wrapping depends on viewport width uses `min-h-4`, which still reserves space unconditionally but cannot clip. A fixed `h-4` under a wrapping sentence overflows into the block below, and the states most likely to carry long copy are first-run states, so the bug ships visible on day one (#306).
- **On form fields, the reserved slot doubles as the helper slot.** Render helper text there by default and swap it for the error when the field is invalid. This satisfies the reservation rule while spending the space productively instead of leaving a dead gap under every input, and it keeps `aria-describedby` pointing at one stable id.
- **Pending server-side effects follow the same rules.** A note describing a deferred/pending server-side effect (e.g. "confirmation email sent") is the affected field's reserved helper slot: render its container unconditionally, with a stable id that the field's `aria-describedby` always points at, and vary only the text. The note itself is not a live region. If the page doesn't navigate away after the triggering save, the save-result line is the terminal state, so make it `role="status" aria-live="polite" aria-atomic="true"`. When part of the change hasn't taken effect, put state-specific copy in that live region instead of a generic "Changes saved." (e.g. "Profile updated. Check your inbox to confirm your new email address."). The two must never coexist (#1273, ProfileTab's pending email change).
- **Actions on a pending effect sit under its note, outside the `<label>`.** Follow-up actions on a pending server-side effect (e.g. **Resend link** / **Cancel change** under ProfileTab's pending email note) are bare-text secondary buttons (`text-xs`, `px-1.5 py-0.5`, `gap-3`, standard focus ring, `type="button"`) rendered only while the effect is pending, placed after the note and outside the field's `<label>` so they never join the input's accessible name; each carries `aria-describedby` pointing at the note so it is announced with what it acts on. Their results go to the same save-result live region as the form's own save (success / `text-warning` for a 429 cooldown whose copy comes from the server / `text-danger`), and a new action or save replaces the previous result — never two at once. When an action ends the pending state and the buttons unmount, move focus to the field they belonged to rather than letting it drop to `<body>`. A personal, reversible withdrawal like Cancel change commits immediately without an inline confirm (#1293).
- **Re-authentication field for a sensitive profile change (#1641).** Show a Current password field only while the sensitive edit is pending (for example, email differs from the saved value) and only for accounts with a usable password. Otherwise omit it from the DOM, never disabled. It uses `type=password`, `autoComplete="current-password"` and `required`, and keeps its state out of the main form object. A helper line states why it is needed. The value clears when the triggering edit is reverted, on success, and on a server `current_password` error. That error renders in the field's helper slot (`text-danger`) with `aria-invalid` and focus moved to the field, not only in the page-level status line.

The canonical `w-5 h-5` overflows a `py-1.5` button. Established by `AutosaveIndicator`, reused by `EmailSettingsSection`.

## First-encounter indicators

When surfacing a feature that users may not discover on their own, use a static dot indicator:

- **Token:** `bg-primary-emphasis rounded-full` — distinct from `bg-primary-soft`, which is reserved for active filter/selection states
- **Size:** `w-2 h-2` minimum — `w-1.5` (6px) is too small to draw attention at desktop scale
- **Position:** `absolute top-0 right-0` inside the button's `relative` wrapper
- **Pointer events:** always `pointer-events-none` — the dot must never intercept clicks intended for the button beneath it
- **Dismissal trigger:** first intentional click/activation of the associated button — not hover (hover is passive and transient)
- **Persistence:** use `user:prefs:{pref-name}` localStorage key with `false` as the "unseen" default (show dot); `true` means seen (hide dot). Follow the try/catch + fallback pattern in `useExportSeenPref.ts`
- **No animation** — no pulsing, no fade-in. A static dot is sufficient and avoids motion-sensitivity concerns
- **Gate it on the same condition as the button** — if the button is hidden for viewers (`canEdit && onMoveCard`), the dot must be hidden too

## Mode indicator banners

**Post-join confirmation (#1444, #2107).** `useJoinedToast` (`Common/useJoinedToast.ts`) raises the single post-join confirmation as a persistent `success` toast (key `joined`, no auto-dismiss), used by `GroupDetail` (#998) and `BoardView` (#1444). It is the only confirmation the join produced, so it has no timer and stays until dismissed or the page is left. It is a `success` toast and so the lowest priority and not protected from eviction; that is accepted (decided #2107): it is raised on landing, when the region is almost always empty, and only a fourth toast arriving while three are visible could evict it. The title is captured once in `useState`, because a tab click navigates without `location.state`. Any new invite surface that lands the user on a destination page reuses it rather than hand-rolling a banner.

**Board search-result notices (#1763).** A transient board-level notice about the search result (e.g. "Showing the first N of M matches") renders between the `FilterBar` and the scroll container, in the same slot as the mode banners, never inside the scroll container. Keep the margin and padding on the inner element so the always-mounted live-region wrapper takes no layout space while empty.

When a persistent board-wide mode is active (e.g. focus mode, a future "view-only" lock), render a full-width strip between the filter row and the scroll container. The strip must sit **outside** the scroll container so it does not scroll away.

- Style: `bg-primary/15 border-b border-primary-emphasis/40 px-4 py-2 flex items-center gap-3 text-sm text-info transition-opacity duration-150`
- Use `bg-primary/15` to signal "active mode" — distinct from transient toasts (§ Toasts: `bg-surface` shells, with the `info` toast's own `bg-primary/15` tint over it) and warnings (amber)
- Exit controls within the strip use the **Quiet button variant** (`text-fg-secondary hover:text-fg hover:bg-surface-hover px-2 py-1 rounded text-xs shrink-0 focus:ring-2 focus:ring-primary-emphasis`) — never the primary variant
- Mode name or target label: `font-medium text-info truncate max-w-[24rem]` with `flex-shrink-0` on the exit button

## Degraded-state site banners (system-imposed, not user-chosen)

Not every persistent banner is a "mode" the current user opted into. When a banner reflects a site-wide degraded state set by an operator — the user did not choose it and cannot exit it — use the amber degraded-state language from § Connection status indicator, **not** the `bg-primary/15` mode-indicator treatment above. `bg-primary/15` reads as "you turned this on"; for a state imposed on the user it reads as decorative and gets ignored.

- Style: `bg-warning/10 border-b border-warning/30 px-4 py-2 flex items-center gap-3 text-sm text-warning shrink-0`
- Sits **outside** the scroll container, same placement rule as § Mode indicator banners, so it never scrolls away
- **Non-dismissible** for the duration of the state — no exit or dismiss control. The banner disappears on its own once the operator clears the condition. A banner the user can dismiss is one they will dismiss and then forget, which defeats the point of telling them
- `role="status" aria-live="polite" aria-atomic="true"` — informational, **not** `role="alert"`. The user did not cause this and has nothing to act on, so it must not interrupt them
- The message text is operator-supplied: render it as a text node, never via `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`. Use `truncate min-w-0 flex-1` plus a `title` so a long notice never wraps the strip or forces horizontal scroll, while staying complete in the DOM for screen readers
- **Icon: use the app's canonical `⚠` / `⛔` glyph, never a bespoke SVG** — `<span aria-hidden="true" className="text-base leading-none shrink-0">⚠</span>`. These are the same Unicode characters `ColumnHeader`, `MoveBlockedToast` and `LensProvenanceBanner` already use for identical amber/red severity semantics; drawing a new SVG triangle per banner gives the app two different renderings of one meaning
- Reference implementation: `MaintenanceBanner` (`src/components/Common/MaintenanceBanner.tsx`, issue #783)

## Inline summary panels (embedded, not chrome)

Not every amber-toned status block is a § Degraded-state site banner. That section is specifically for persistent, full-width chrome outside the scroll container (`MaintenanceBanner`). When a panel instead sits *inside* a form or card to summarize the live effect of settings the user is actively editing — e.g. Admin Settings → Email's "Currently sending mail" panel (`EmailSettingsSection.tsx`) — it may borrow the same tokens (`border-warning/30 bg-warning/10`, the canonical `⚠`/`⛔` glyph, `aria-hidden` on the glyph) but must **not** copy the `role="status" aria-live="polite"` treatment.

- Use `role="group" aria-labelledby="{heading-id}"` instead.
- **Why:** these panels sit next to an action that already owns a live region for *its* result (Save, Send test). A second concurrent polite region queues against the first and produces duplicated or out-of-order announcements. Carry exactly one live region per user-initiated action, not one per visually-amber element.
- State the panel's facts from **saved server state only**, never the in-progress draft — the panel answers "what is happening right now", which an uncommitted edit has not changed.
- Warning copy in such a panel is usually a full sentence, so its reserved slot uses `min-h-4`, not `h-4`. See § Inline status messages.

## De-emphasized controls and context-dependent defaults (#1063)

- **De-emphasis must not drop below AA.** To visually demote an enabled control (e.g. the Board tab on a lens-only board), use `text-fg-secondary italic` and keep the hover state. Never use `text-fg-muted` or `text-fg-tertiary` on a `bg-surface-hover` track (the `ViewToggle` background) — in dark mode they are 3.32:1 and 4.04:1 even after the #2030 retune, which made `fg-muted` AA only on resting surfaces (§ Color tokens). A de-emphasized control stays enabled, focusable, one click away, keeps the standard focus ring, and explains itself via `Tooltip`; never `disabled`, `pointer-events-none`, or hidden. The de-emphasis condition is derived automatically (lens configured AND zero native swimlanes), never a per-board setting.
- **A default view that depends on data resolved elsewhere yields to explicit intent.** The lens is the default landing tab only when `?view=` is absent and there is no `?card=` / `?focus=` deep link; an explicit `?view=` always wins, and opening a native card (deep link or command palette) switches to the Board view because the lens branch renders no `CardDetail`.
- **Unenforced settings.** A setting that is stored and editable but not yet applied by the backend must carry a visible helper line directly beneath its control: `text-xs text-fg-muted mt-2`, starting with "Not yet enforced." followed by one sentence stating the actual current behavior. Wire it with `aria-describedby` where the control supports it (`SelectDropdown` takes it as the `ariaDescribedBy` prop). Never hide or disable the control to signal this. Remove the hint in the same MR that wires enforcement. A setting the maintainers have deprecated instead of wiring (e.g. the group default member role, #1430) keeps its control and swaps the lead word to "Deprecated." (`Deprecated. This setting has no effect: <actual behavior>.`), with the same placement and `aria-describedby` wiring. Section intro copy must not promise behavior that any control in the section does not deliver. List each lead word in use ("Not yet enforced", "Deprecated") in the section intro only while a control in that section carries it.

## Inline informational callouts (login demo banner, #1034)

An informational callout inside a card or form (not a full-width chrome strip) uses `role="note"` — static, not announced — with `rounded border border-primary-emphasis/30 bg-primary-emphasis/10 px-3 py-2.5 text-sm text-fg-secondary`. It is a deliberate deviation from § Mode indicator banners / § Degraded-state site banners, which are chrome strips. Copyable values (credentials, IDs) go in `<code className="font-mono text-fg select-all">` so one click selects them. **Superseded (#1179):** the reset time is no longer hardcoded — it is interpolated from site-config's `demo_next_reset_at` via `formatClockTime()`; see § Hosted demo surfaces.

**Neutral variant (#2298):** a note that explains state rather than promotes something uses `role="note"` with `rounded-lg border border-line bg-sunken px-3 py-3 text-sm text-fg-secondary` and a `text-fg-tertiary` icon (`WebhookServiceNotice`, `IntakeColumnCallout`, the Add Column take-over note). Body text inside it stays `text-fg-secondary`, never `fg-tertiary`, for dark-mode contrast. An optional disclosure inside it is a native `<button type="button">` with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` pointing at a `hidden`-toggled region (not conditionally rendered, so the id always resolves). Never put the `hidden` attribute on an element that also has a `display` utility (`flex`, `grid`): Tailwind preflight's `[hidden]` rule is not `!important`, so the utility wins and the region stays visible; put `hidden` on a wrapper and the utility on a child. jsdom cannot see this, so the e2e/browser check is `toBeHidden`. A note shown in more than one modal shares one component (icon and container), not copied markup.

## Transient state cues (#2251)

A cue that appears in response to a user filter or setting change (for example "doesn't match lane filters" in the focus banner) renders inside a persistent `role="status"` element with only its text conditional; a live region mounted together with its content is not reliably announced. Keep the cue at `text-xs` or larger, mark separator glyphs `aria-hidden`, and add `shrink-0 whitespace-nowrap` so a truncating neighbor shrinks instead.

**Focus banner text contrast (#2252).** The swimlane focus banner is a `bg-primary/15` tint over the board page. Its tones are `text-info-on-tint` ("Focused on:" and the lane name), the `text-xs text-fg-tertiary` status cue and the `text-fg-secondary` Exit focus button (hover `text-fg` on its own opaque `bg-surface-hover`). Measured on the tint over canvas, sunken and surface, the worst is `fg-tertiary`: 4.99:1 dark (over a surface backdrop; 7.04 over canvas) and 5.65 light, so no tint-scoped token is needed. `onTintTokens.test.ts` parses the banner's text colors and alpha from `BoardView.tsx` and pins that floor. A new tone in the banner must clear 4.5:1 on the tint in both themes, and plain `text-fg-muted` does not (3.99 light over canvas).

### Read-only informational checkbox and column-settings "More" (#2299)

- A checkbox whose state is fixed by the system (checked and `disabled`) uses native `disabled`, `accent-primary`, and `disabled:opacity-40 disabled:cursor-not-allowed` on the input only. Never put a whole-label opacity modifier on it; the reason is a visible sibling line referenced by `aria-describedby`.
- `MoreDisclosure` (in `IntakeColumnCallout.tsx`) is the one collapsed-by-default More/Less disclosure for column-settings notes. Reuse it; keep `hidden` on the wrapper div.
